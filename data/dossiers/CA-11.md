# CA-11: Scott Wiener (D) vs Connie Chan (D), open seat (Pelosi retiring), same-party top-two

**Kalshi (2026-11-01 snapshot):** Wiener 0.795 mid (0.787 normalized), Chan 0.215.
**Judgment:** the market is about right. q(Chan) = 0.23. Confidence: medium.

## Polls
- USC Democracy and Fair Elections Lab (independent), Oct 16-19 2026, 454 LV, +/-4.6, five languages: Wiener 47, Chan 41, undecided 9. With undecided voters pushed to choose: Wiener 51, Chan 44. Asian/Pacific Islander respondents favor Wiener 59-31. Sources: SF Standard, 2026-10-22, https://sfstandard.com/2026/10/22/poll-chan-trailing-wiener-despite-pelosi-support/ and Mission Local, 2026-10-25, https://missionlocal.org/?p=879413
- Tavern Research (D, for Jobs and Democracy PAC), Sep 15-17, 658 LV: Wiener 45, Chan 34 (via votehub; Politico CA Playbook, 2026-10-02).
- Earlier: Lake Research (Chan internal) had Wiener 45-40. A late-June housing-committee poll had Wiener 39-38.

## Ratings
- Not applicable: both candidates are Democrats, and every rater has it Safe D.

## Money
- FEC filings through Oct 14: Wiener raised $5.81M and has $0.61M cash on hand. Chan raised $2.40M and has $0.58M on hand. I found no outside-spending totals.

## News
- Pelosi endorsed Chan in spring 2026, and Gary Locke backs her. USC's Grose says Pelosi's endorsement "is not translating to Chan support" and that Wiener's voters are likelier to turn out in a midterm (SF Standard, 2026-10-22).
- Mission Local (2026-10-25) argues that monolingual Chinese-speaking voters, who may be underpolled, could boost Chan.

## Structure
- California top-two, so both general-election candidates are Democrats. In the June 2 primary, Wiener took 40.7%, Chan 29.7% and Chakrabarti 17.9%; the remaining ~12% includes Republicans (https://richmondsunsetnews.com/2026/07/09/chan-and-wiener-advance-to-novembers-general-election-for-pelosis-seat/). Chan's path is to consolidate the left (DSA and Chakrabarti voters), lower-income voters and anti-Wiener Republicans. USC finds DSA voters and Republicans leaning Chan. The lines are unchanged and the PVI is D+36. San Francisco counts slowly, but that does not affect who wins.

## Reasoning
Wiener led every poll I found, by 5 to 11 points. The one independent poll has him +6/+7, and the midterm turnout profile favors his voters. Chan has a real but narrow path through left-plus-Republican consolidation and possible underpolling of Chinese-speaking voters. Polling error in a same-party local race is large, which is why I keep her around 20-25%. Kalshi is at 21%, so I see no clear mispricing.
