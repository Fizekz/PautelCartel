## Judgment
The seat is Trump+13 on the new lines. A roughly 10-point national swing from 2024 still leaves Luna slightly ahead, and Florida has trended R relative to the nation. Every public poll is partisan; the Democratic-sponsored ones show about a tie, and a D-aligned Oct 22 poll has Luna +3. Democrats are outspending Republicans on outside money, which is a real factor. Historically, R incumbents rated Lean R by all three raters won 11 of 12 in 2018. I put Luna at about 69%.

**Errors**

- Outside-money figures conflict between sources. Yahoo (Oct 29) gives Democratic outside money as more than $4.5M and Republican groups (Freedom Caucus Fund, MAHA PAC) at about $1.6M. PoliAgg says $6.4M and $3.5M. PoliAgg's breakdown is also unreliable: it lists VoteVets, a Democratic group, on the R side. Cite a range, not a precise split.

**Missed evidence**
- Uniform swing: Harris took 43.3% two-party on the new lines (lean_2026.csv). About 5 points of swing gives about 48.4%, so Luna is ahead by about 3 before incumbency. In 2024 she won the old lines by about 9.6 (Fox at 45.2% two-party, results_house.csv). Yahoo implies Trump won them by about 11, so she ran slightly behind Trump and has no personal-vote cushion. That supports Luna as favourite but not overwhelmingly.
- A D-aligned pollster's last release (RedBox, Oct 22) shows Luna +3. Under the usual 2-4 point sponsor bias, the true margin would be Luna +5 to +7. This is the strongest race-specific point for "underrates favourite", and the dossier underplays it.
- As in CO-03, Kalshi prices all eight Lean-R-by-all-three Republican incumbents at 0.50-0.69. Luna at 0.625 is not an outlier on the board.
