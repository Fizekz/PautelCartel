## Structure
- Plurality election, two candidates, 2024 lines.
- Trump won the district 58.6-41.4 (two-party). Cook PVI R+8. Spartz won 56.6% in 2024.
- The district takes in the fast-diversifying, college-educated suburbs north of Indianapolis (Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville) plus Muncie, Marion and Kokomo.

## Reasoning
The fundamentals point to roughly R+7 in a D+9 environment (2024 presidential margin minus the national swing). The polls point to R+1 to R+3, and the Republican-leaning Quantus poll shows the same close race.

A blend lands around R+4 to R+5. With the usual 6-7 point error in House polling, that gives Ford about 22-27%.

The October rating moves are specific, recent evidence pointing the same way, especially Cook's move to Lean R on Oct 30. Market prices often lag such moves. I put Ford at about 24%, against the market's 16.5%.

The risk is that both D polls lean toward Democrats and that Indiana's fundamentals hold.

## Skeptic review
_Independent check, 2026-11-01. Web search was used up after two queries, so this review relies on WebFetch, current Wikipedia wikitext pulled by API (IN page rev 1383067073 is still current, dated 2026-10-31T20:29Z), and the repo data files._

**Verdict: weakened.** The direction (Ford somewhat underpriced) is plausible, but the 24% estimate overstates the evidence. My q_underdog for Ford is **0.20**, against the market's 0.165.

**Claims verified**
- **Wikipedia lists the three polls exactly as the dossier states them.** Quantus (R) Oct 21-22, 601 LV, ±4.4: 45-42, and 47-45 with leaners; source dated Oct 27. Expedition (D) for HMP, Sep 30-Oct 4, 600 LV: 48-47; source dated Oct 21. Impact (D) for Ford, Jul 16-20, 400 LV: 48-46.
- **Two of the three polls cannot be read at the source.**
  - The Quantus page (https://quantusinsights.org/polling/f/indiana-5th-spartz-leads-ford-by-3-in-tight-race) shows only the headline "Spartz Leads Ford by 3 in Tight Race."
  - The Expedition poll is known only from a tweet that returns HTTP 402.
- **Ratings, Sep 29 vs Nov 1, all confirmed** from the wikitext of revs 1377510186 and 1383160783. Cook moved to Lean R on Oct 30.
- **FEC figures (through Oct 14) match** fec_2026.csv.

**Missed evidence (anti-Ford)**
- **Primary turnout showed no Democratic surge.** In the May 5 primary, 41,404 votes were cast on the D side vs 56,221 on the R side (Wikipedia IN rev 1383067073). That is a 42.4% D share against Trump's 41.4% D two-party share, so the D side ran only about 1 point ahead of the 2024 baseline. NC-11 ran about 7.5 points ahead. Both IN-05 primaries were contested.
- **Ford's own internal still shows R+5 on partisanship.** The July Impact Research poll had an in-district generic ballot of R 45, D 40 (Larry in Fishers, Aug 6 2026: https://www.larryinfishers.com/2026/08/06/spartz-ford-5th-district-poll/). Ford's six-point lead appeared only after respondents heard positive messaging about both candidates. Ford running ahead of the generic ballot depends on Spartz-specific weakness holding until Nov 3.
- **The pollster that ran the Emerson district batch expects seats like this to hold.** Poll Vault's Oct 22 preview (https://pollvault.substack.com/p/levee-math) says seats at R+6 or redder will likely hold. IN-05 is R+8.
- **Most raters stay at Likely R:** IE, Sabato, DDHQ, FPO, Fox and Silver Bulletin. Kalshi's 0.165 came after Cook's Oct 30 move, so that rating change is already in the price.

**Missed evidence (pro-Ford)**
- **IN-05 is cheap relative to its rating peers.** Among R-held seats rated about Lean/Likely R (mean rating 1.3-2.0), Kalshi prices the D at 0.245-0.385 in FL-07, MO-02, TX-09 and CO-04. IN-05 is at 0.165, the cheapest in that bucket except AK-AL.
- **The DCCC put IN-05 on its "Districts in Play" list** in early August (Larry in Fishers, Aug 6 2026).
- **Spartz is a weak incumbent.** The Washington Examiner (Sep 25 2026, https://washingtonexaminer.com/premium/4729924/indiana-5th-district-ford-spartz-competitive/) cites her erratic record, staff turnover and workplace-abuse accusations. It also notes that the only public poll at that time was Ford's internal.
- **Not found:** HMP/DCCC independent-expenditure totals (the FEC API was rate-limited and OpenSecrets is blocked), debates, and early-vote numbers.

**Unreliable source to avoid:** https://uspollingdata.com/news/house-2026-indiana-races/ calls IN-5 a "Toss-up." It also falsely claims Democrats came within 3 points in 2022 (Spartz won 61%). Do not use it.

**Net:** A modest underdog edge (about 20% vs 16.5%) is defensible, but it rests on two polls I could not verify at the source plus a single R pollster. Treat it as low-to-medium confidence, not 24%.
