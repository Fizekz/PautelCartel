## Structure
Texas 2026 new map. On the new lines Trump won ~57.5% 2p (~+15). Independent Ben Mendoza (El Paso) is also on the ballot. Heavily Hispanic, border district. The seat has been vacant since Gonzales resigned in Apr 2026 after a staffer sex scandal.

## Judgement
A uniform D+8.5 swing from a Trump+15 seat leaves about R+5. Hispanic-area reversion and Herrera's weakness as a candidate push it closer, and Kalshi's own margin market implied ~R+1.3 (Oct 27). DDHQ's model (73%) and the raters (Lean/Likely R) are more bullish on Herrera than the market is. The R money edge also points that way. On balance I put Herrera at ~0.58. That is not far enough from 0.54 to call the market wrong with confidence.

**Assessment**
The only recent poll is a D-sponsored tie. After a sponsor adjustment that is roughly Herrera +3, consistent with DDHQ's R+6 model and the market's R+1.3. Herrera's baggage is real and well publicised, but the seat is Trump +15.

A 0.54 price sits below DDHQ (0.73) and Polymarket (0.59 on Oct 27), so if anything it leans slightly toward Stout. The gap is not large enough, and not backed by enough public evidence, to call a mispricing. I agree with the dossier's Herrera estimate of about 0.56-0.58.
