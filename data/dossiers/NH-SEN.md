## Skeptic review (2026-11-01)
**Verdict: upheld (market_about_right). Skeptic q_underdog (Sununu) = 0.07.**

Note: the shared WebSearch budget was used up. This review uses direct fetches only. WMUR blocked fetching, so I could not establish the outcome of the Oct 28 WMUR debate, or whether it happened.

**Claims checked**
- These polls match the Wikipedia table (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_New_Hampshire, accessed 2026-11-01): Rasmussen Oct 20-23 (50-44), Saint Anselm Oct 19-20 (50-43), NPA Oct 8-12 (50-45 / 50-43), YouGov (51-43), Rasmussen Oct 2-4 (49-44), Saint Anselm/AARP (51-44), Trafalgar (47-41), NYT/Siena (50-45), UNH (50-42) and InsiderAdvantage (48-40).
- UMass Lowell/YouGov is confirmed by its press release dated Oct 28, 2026: field dates Oct 2-19, 600 LV, Pappas 48, Sununu 41, LaPlante 4, MoE ±5 (https://www.uml.edu/news/press-releases/2026/102826-new-england-polls.aspx).
- FEC numbers match: Pappas $23.70M raised, $1.98M cash on hand; Sununu $5.68M raised, $1.71M cash on hand.
- The ratings match the table. Wikipedia also lists CBS News at Lean D (Oct 17).

**Errors**
- "Every poll since the September 8 primary shows Pappas ahead by 5 to 8" is **false**. Wikipedia lists these September polls:
  - co/efficient (R), Sep 9-11, 958 LV: **46-46 tie**
  - Rasmussen, Sep 8-10: Pappas 45-34 (+11)
  - InsiderAdvantage and UNH: +8 each
- The statement does hold for every poll with fieldwork in October (PollCheck lists all at +5 to +8).
- The Boston Globe (Oct 22, 2026) says debates or forums were held **Oct 13 in Conway and Oct 14 in Salem**, and that both campaigns had confirmed the WMUR debate on Oct 28 (https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/10/22/metro/nh-senate-debate-nhpr-canceled/). The dossier implies there was no earlier debate. It was also Pappas who declined a separate debate at New England College.

**Missed evidence**
- PollCheck (updated 1 Nov 2026): Pappas 49.5, Sununu 42.6, **83% Pappas win probability**, Likely D (https://www.pollcheck.co.uk/us-midterms-2026/senate/new-hampshire). That is somewhat below Kalshi's 92.5%.
- Wikipedia's aggregate of five averages through Oct 20 is Pappas +7.0.
- Boston 25 (Oct 26, 2026) reports that the Saint Anselm director says Pappas has led since spring and that frustration with Trump is helping him. No late shift was reported.

**Assessment**
The race has been stable at about +7 for six weeks, with a 4x money edge and a D+2 state lean. No evidence of a late break toward Sununu. A simple poll model gives Sununu about 17%, but those models are typically underconfident on stable races in a favourable environment. I put Sununu at about 7%, which gives no edge.
