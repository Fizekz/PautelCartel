## Judgment
This is the one race in the set where several kinds of evidence disagree with Kalshi:
- The only recent poll has Bouchard +5, from a Democratic-aligned sponsor.
- Cook and Sabato have it at Tossup and Inside Elections at Tilt R.
- An independent model gives Bouchard 70%.
- Kalshi's jump to 0.76 came on thin volume in the final 48 hours.

The case for Hines is real but more modest:
- Michigan's environment is clearly D-leaning (El-Sayed +4 to +7). Applied uniformly, that puts the district near even.
- Hines has the fundraising edge.
- The poll-light quantitative raters (Silver Bulletin, Split Ticket, DDHQ) say Lean D.

Weighing all of this, Hines is about a 55-62% favourite, not 76%. That puts Bouchard around 0.38-0.45. I use 0.40.

## Skeptic review (2026-11-01)

**Verdict: weakened.** The claim's direction holds: Bouchard is more likely than his 0.245 price implies. But the 0.40 figure leans too hard on one text-to-web poll from a super PAC's in-house pollster, and on a model that this one poll moved. **Skeptic q_underdog (Bouchard) = 0.35.**

**Errors and overstatements in the dossier**
- The cross-check is read too kindly. RedBox's own statewide poll has El-Sayed +5. Shifting that by the 2024 lean gap (5.3 points, from Trump +6.7 in the district vs Trump +1.4 statewide) predicts about Rogers +0.3 in MI-10. The district sample has Rogers +2.4, so it leans about 2 points R relative to the same firm's statewide sample. That is a modest R tilt, not "not obviously skewed."
- The crosstabs look odd. Independents favour Hines 44.7-34.1, yet she trails by 4.8 overall. Women favour Bouchard 46.7-44.9, a very small gender gap for a D-leaning midterm (same source). Neither is disqualifying, but both argue for caution with a single poll.
- The point that a Democratic-aligned sponsor makes an R result more credible is weaker here. A Fight Worth Having is a progressive super PAC polling with its own in-house unit. It released district polls showing establishment-backed Democrats trailing in both MI-10 and MI-04 (Huizenga +3; see MI-04.md). Its incentives are not straightforwardly pro-Hines.
- Wikipedia's predictions table is stale (Silver Bulletin Lean D as of Aug 15; Economist Likely D (flip) as of May 22). The ratings_2026.csv snapshot (Oct 30) is the better source, and the dossier uses it correctly.

**Evidence the dossier missed (both directions)**
- Pro-Bouchard: in the Aug 10-13 GSG/HMP poll, Hines and Bouchard tie 45-45, but the generic ballot in the same poll is D 46-42. That makes two polls (GSG and RedBox) in which Hines runs about 2-4 points behind her party. Wikipedia District 10 polling table (fetched Nov 1, 2026), link above.
- Pro-Hines: Kalshi already discounts RedBox's district polls. MI-04 (Trump +5.6, R incumbent) has McCann (D) at 0.615 despite RedBox's Huizenga +3. Comparable Trump +5-8 R-held seats rated Lean D (flip) are priced higher than MI-10: PA-10 at 0.815 and WI-03 at 0.885 (Kalshi snapshot races_2026-11-01T074812Z.csv). So the market is already pricing MI-10 as more R than its peers, which cuts against "Kalshi ignores the R evidence."
- Pro-Hines: Hines has out-raised Bouchard ($4.16M vs $2.88M) and is in DCCC Red to Blue. Late statewide polls are D+5 to D+9 (CNN/SSRS D+7, Mitchell D+7, MSU/Verasight D+9.2; https://www.callthemap.com/polls/SEN-MI, Oct 31, 2026). A uniform swing at that level puts the district near even to slightly D.

**Net:** fundamentals put the race near a tossup, and the polling (two polls with Hines lagging her party) leans slightly R. Hines at 76% is too high, but Bouchard at 40% needs more than one super-PAC text poll. I put Bouchard at 0.35, with low confidence.
