## Structure
Plurality vote, no runoff. Libertarian Graham polls 2-7%. Kansas leans R+8 (Trump 58.2% two-party in 2024). Past Kansas Senate polls underestimated Republicans: 2014 Orman, 2020 Marshall beat his polls by about 7. Kansas governor races in 2018 and 2022 went the other way.

## Reasoning
Every high-quality nonpartisan poll in the last three weeks has Hamilton ahead by 3-9, and he has a 3.5x hard-money edge. The market's 50/50 already prices in Kansas's partisan lean and the history of polls missing toward the GOP. My read is that this discounts a consistent Hamilton lead (about +3 to +4) too much in a D+8 environment. Still, the R+8 lean and the 2014/2020 misses are real, so the edge is small: P(Hamilton) is about 0.58, P(Marshall) about 0.42.

## Skeptic review
*Independent skeptic, 2026-11-01. Web search budget ran out partway through; checks used WebFetch of specific pages.*

**Verdict: upheld (low confidence).** Shading toward Hamilton is supported, but by less than the dossier's 0.58.

**Checks**
- Wikipedia's KS-SEN poll table, read 2026-11-01, matches every poll the dossier cites: Emerson Oct 26-28 49-45 (50-47 with leaners), WaPo/SSPG Oct 22-26 52-43 (54-44 two-way), SSRS Oct 20-26 49-45, RedBox (D) 46-45 Marshall, InsiderAdvantage (R) 48-47 / 47-48, NYT/Siena 45-45, Trafalgar 44-44.
- Wikipedia's aggregator table (through Oct 28) has Hamilton +2.6 to +3.8: 270toWin +3.8, DDHQ +2.9, FiftyPlusOne +3.3, RCP +2.6, Silver Bulletin +3.1, average +3.2.
- Ratings in the dossier also match: Silver Bulletin Lean D (Oct 30); CBS, Fox and MS NOW Lean R; the rest Tossup.

**Errors and overstatements**
- "Every high-quality nonpartisan poll in the last three weeks": the YouGov poll (Sep 24-Oct 4) ended four weeks ago, not within three.
- Within the last three weeks there are really three nonpartisan polls: WaPo, SSRS and Emerson. NYT/Siena, the highest-rated, is from late September and was tied.
- The WaPo +9 is the outlier. KWCH reported (2026-10-30) that the Post acknowledged reaching fewer Republican than Democratic respondents. Emporia State's Michael Smith said Republican responses should get extra weight and the poll should be checked against others as a possible outlier (https://www.kwch.com/2026/10/31/new-poll-places-adam-hamilton-leader-race-us-senate/). Leaving WaPo out, the recent nonpartisan margin is about +4 (SSRS, Emerson).

**Missed evidence**
- Wikipedia (read 2026-11-01, item undated) says Trump used an antisemitic slur at a Marshall rally. Barbara Comstock criticised it. The effect on the race is unknown.
- Kansas City Star, via Wikipedia (undated): both campaigns made exaggerated or false claims about Epstein and the Church of the Resurrection abuse case.
- Oct 21 debate issues, via Wikipedia: Marshall defended the tariffs and continued operations in the Iran war. Hamilton called for an exit.
- I found no statewide advance-ballot party split I could verify. The Substack's figure of Republicans +35K is still unverified.

**Skeptic view**
- With a polling lead of about 3 and a Senate polling-error SD of about 5-6, Hamilton would be about 70% if the polls are unbiased.
- Kansas's polling misses have run in both directions. Republicans beat their polls in 2014 and 2020; Democrats beat theirs in the 2018 and 2022 governor races and the 2022 abortion amendment. A 2-3 point Republican-bias haircut is therefore reasonable, not more.
- The market is deep (about $7.7M volume), which argues for humility.
- P(Hamilton) is about 0.56 and P(Marshall) about 0.44.
