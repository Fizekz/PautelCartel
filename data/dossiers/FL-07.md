# FL-07: Ryan Elijah (R) vs Bale Dalton (D), open seat

**Kalshi, Nov 1:** Elijah (REP) 0.655, Dalton (DEM) 0.345. Event volume is about $111k.
**Judgement:** market about right. q(Dalton) = 0.32. Confidence: medium.

## Structure
- The seat is open. Incumbent Cory Mills lost the August GOP primary to Elijah, a former Orlando TV anchor.
- Florida enacted a new map in 2026, but FL-07's geography is unchanged per lean_2026: identical 2024 vote totals.
- 2024 presidential vote was Trump 56.3 / Harris 43.7 two-party (about Trump +12.6). PVI is R+5.
- Libertarian Christopher Dennison is on the ballot. The race is plurality, not RCV.

## Polls
- **DCCC Analytics (internal D), Oct 17-19, 414 LV:** Dalton 45, Elijah 45, 10% undecided. In the same poll the generic ballot was R+3 (44 D / 47 R), Trump approval 38/52, recalled 2024 vote Trump +9 (actual about +12.6, so the sample leans D), and 55% could not rate Elijah. Source: https://dccc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/FL-07-Polling-Memo.pdf (memo dated Oct 20, 2026).
- **PPP (D), May 20-21, 570 RV:** in data/clean/polls_2026.csv.
- I found no independent public poll.
- For context, Florida statewide Senate polls in late October run Moody (R) +4 to +8 (NYT/Siena Oct 25: 50-44), against Trump +13 in 2024.

A tracker that may be unreliable had Seminole D ahead about 30k to 27k as of Oct 26.

## Reasoning
- The only poll is a D internal showing a tie. Its own generic ballot is R+3, and its sample recalls a 2024 vote about 3.5 points bluer than the actual result. That points to Elijah narrowly ahead.
- The national environment is D+8 to D+9, with Trump approval around 37%. Florida statewide swing (about 6-8 points from 2024) puts this seat at roughly R+4 to R+6. That is partly offset by Dalton's money and profile and by Elijah being little known.
- The HMP pull-back is a negative signal from Democrats' own data. Cook's Oct 30 move is a mild positive.
- Net: about 30-35% Dalton, in line with the market.
