Cross-check: on fundamentals the dossier is, if anything, too favourable to Moody. Trump took 56.6% of the two-party vote (lean_2026.csv), about R+13 against a national R+1.6. A uniform swing to national D+9 gives only about Moody +2, yet polls show +6. The gap can be read either way: as Florida's continued rightward registration trend, or as room for late Democratic movement. Florida's polling misses in 2018-2024 ran toward Republicans, so the polls are the better guide. Twenty-plus polls agree on +4 to +11, with a single +2 (a Nixon internal, and St. Pete in early October). With about 6% undecided, a 6-point lead and a statewide error SD of about 4.5, Nixon's chance is 8-12%. No reason to depart from the market.
