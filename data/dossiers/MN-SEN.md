## Skeptic review (2026-11-01)
**Verdict: upheld (market_about_right). Skeptic q_underdog (Tafoya) = 0.09.**

Note: the shared WebSearch budget was used up when this review began. This review rests on direct fetches of Wikipedia, PollCheck and the dossier's Newsweek link, not on fresh searches. Late local news (Star Tribune, MPR, MN Reformer returned 403) was not checked.

**Errors and omissions**
- Small error: the dossier dates the PollCheck average to Oct 27. PollCheck now shows an update dated 1 Nov 2026: Flanagan 48.3, Tafoya 42.1, with an **81% Flanagan win probability** (https://www.pollcheck.co.uk/us-midterms-2026/senate/minnesota). That is a little below Kalshi's 90.7%, so one simple poll model leans slightly toward Tafoya relative to the market.
- Missed: the RCP average was Flanagan +2.6 in mid-October (Newsweek) and +5.1 by about Oct 26 (Wikipedia). The race has moved toward Flanagan, not away from her.
- Missed: MS NOW rates the race Lean D (Oct 12, Wikipedia).
- Missed: Tafoya is endorsed by the Minneapolis Police Officers Federation, the MN Farm Bureau PAC and the MPPOA (Wikipedia).

**Assessment**
The narrowest results are R-firm polls at D+3. The two latest high-quality polls show D+7 (SurveyUSA) and D+14 (NYT/Siena), and the averages are D+5 to D+8. The structural lean is D+3 (Cook PVI, lean_2026.csv). I found nothing that supports a Tafoya upset call. I put her at about 9%, essentially the market price. There is no edge in picking her.
