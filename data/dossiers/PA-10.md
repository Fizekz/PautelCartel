## Reasoning
On fundamentals, a uniform swing (2024 House R+2.6 to roughly D+8.5 now) turns Perry's ~1-point win into a high-single-digit Stelson lead. That is the market's case. The district polls are much tighter:
- The only gold-standard poll (NYT/Siena) is tied.
- Two partisan-tagged polls (Susquehanna, RedBox) and F&M show Stelson +2 to +3.
- RCP moved to Tossup, and the Economist and Split Ticket models went from Likely to Lean D.

An expected Stelson margin of ~+3 with ~5–6 points of district error gives about 70–75% for Stelson, a little below 81.5%. Perry at 18.5¢ looks slightly cheap. The edge is small and depends on weighting NYT/Siena heavily, so confidence is low.

**Missed evidence (against reading the Siena tie as a house effect or a strong signal)**
- The same NYT/Inquirer/Siena wave (Oct 16-22, n=400 each) polled three other PA seats (Wikipedia PA House oldid 1382925084, via polls_2026.csv). It was as Democratic as RedBox or more so in each:
  - PA-07: Siena D+5, RedBox D+5.
  - PA-08: Siena D+5, RedBox D+3.
  - PA-01: Siena D+2, RedBox tie.
  - So Siena is not systematically R-leaning here, and the PA-10 tie is most likely sampling noise. With n=400 the margin of error on the gap is about 11 points.
- Perry ran about 4 points behind Trump in 2024: Perry +1.3 against Trump +5.3 two-party (results_house.csv, lean_2026.csv). That is a candidate-specific weakness pointing toward Stelson.
- The market already discounts PA-10 relative to its peers. Kalshi has PA-07 Brooks at 0.865 and PA-08 Cognetti at 0.885, where polls average D+4 to D+5. PA-10's polls average about D+2, and it is priced at 0.815.
  - A blend of polls (+2) and fundamentals (high single digits for D in a D+8 to D+9 environment), with about 5.5 points of error, gives Stelson about 76-80%.
- New negative-ad thread: a Pennsylvania Capital-Star article headlined "Scott Perry ad targeting Janelle Stelson makes unsubstantiated allegations of public nudity" (https://penncapital-star.com/election-2026/scott-perry-ad-targeting-janelle-stelson-makes-unsubstantiated-allegations-of-public-nudity/). I could not fetch it, and its date is unknown. Its effect cannot be assessed.

**Bottom line:** Perry is maybe 3-4 cents cheap, not 7-8. The case rests on one n=400 poll, which cross-district evidence suggests is noise, and on RCP, a rater known to lean R.
