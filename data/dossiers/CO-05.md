Pro-Killin: HMP ~$700k, American Bridge ~$500k, Women Vote ~$500k (https://coloradosun.com/2026/10/28/colorados-5th-congressional-district-is-taking-center-stage/).

## News / early vote
- All-mail state. In El Paso County, ~121k ballots had been returned as of Oct 26: 43% unaffiliated, 32% R, 23% D. Unaffiliated voters are 53% of the district electorate (Colorado Sun, Oct 28). I found no district-level comparison with 2022 or 2024.
- Oct 24: Killin rally with two members of Congress and AG Weiser. Crank is keeping a low profile; his team has attacked Killin's D.C. home.
- History: no Democrat has topped 41% in the district. Crank won 54.7-40.9 in 2024.

## Structure
2024 lines (Colorado Springs / El Paso County). Trump 54.7% 2p (~+9), Cook PVI R+5. Constitution and Libertarian candidates are on the ballot and may draw from Crank. Crank is a first-term incumbent.

## Judgement
Fundamentals (R+9 seat, D+8.5 nation, incumbent) give roughly R+1 to R+3. The D-sponsored polls show a tie or Killin +2, which after a sponsor adjustment means a narrow Crank edge. Outside money favours Crank about 2.5:1, and Killin has the larger campaign war chest. A 55/45 price is reasonable.
