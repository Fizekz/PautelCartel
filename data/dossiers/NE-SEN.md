**Kalshi Nov 1:** Ricketts 0.745, Osborn 0.255, Burbank (D, withdrawn) 0.003. Underdog: Osborn.
**Judgement:** market about right. q_underdog (Osborn) = 0.26. Confidence: medium.

## Structure
- Plurality vote with no Democratic nominee. Cindy Burbank filed to withdraw on 07-17, and the SoS confirmed it on 07-21. She is off the ballot, so a Kalshi price of about 0 is correct.

## Reasoning
The polling average is about Ricketts +2. The latest and best poll (NYT/Siena) has him +6, and even Osborn's own Impact poll has Ricketts ahead. In 2024 Osborn polled close to Deb Fischer but lost by about 6.5 points (background from memory, not re-sourced here). That suggests independent-vs-R polling in Nebraska overstates the independent. The D+9 national environment helps Osborn, but this is a different kind of race from a partisan one. Overall 74.5% for Ricketts looks right, and I see no specific evidence that the market is wrong.
