## Skeptic review
Reviewer: independent skeptic, 2026-11-01. WebSearch budget was exhausted, so I checked claims with direct fetches and raw Wikipedia wikitext.

**Verdict: weakened. The direction (Overman priced a little high) is plausible, but the gap is small and the dossier's evidence summary contains errors. My q_underdog (Overman) = 0.10.**

**Factual errors**
- The ratings are mislabeled. Comparing Wikipedia ratings revisions 1377510186 (Sep 29) and 1383160783 (Nov 1, 08:10 UTC):
  - The only change was **DDHQ, Safe R to Likely R**, not the Economist. The Economist was already Likely R on Sep 29.
  - Current ratings: Cook Solid, Inside Solid, **Fox Solid**, **Split Ticket Safe**. Sabato, Economist, DDHQ, **FiftyPlusOne**, Silver and RCP are at Likely R.
  - The dossier had Split Ticket and Fox at Likely and FiftyPlusOne at Solid. Repo ratings_2026.csv agrees with my reading.
- Revision 1383158409 is dated Nov 1, not Oct 30.

**Checks**
- Primary: Overman 21,833 (52.7%) to McAuley 19,626 (47.3%) (Wikipedia FL page, rev 1383070756). FEC through Oct 14: Bilirakis $2.26M raised / $0.25M cash on hand; Overman $0.28M / $0.03M (repo fec_2026.csv).
- Call the Map: 95%, R+10.1, no poll average (https://www.callthemap.com/house/fl-12, Oct 31). Its money data runs only through Jul 29 and its national inputs through Oct 14.
- PoliAgg's FL-12 page still names McAuley as the nominee and gives R+38.4 (https://poliagg.com/race/us-house-2026-fl-12/, Oct 31). It is broken for this race; don't use it.
- I found no Overman vs Bilirakis poll either.

**Missed evidence**
- *Supports the dossier.* Florida statewide polls show the state well right of the nation (repo polls_2026.csv):
  - NYT/Siena, Oct 19-25, 813 LV: Moody 50-44.
  - UNF, Oct 12-19, 1,471 LV: 49-45.
  - St. Pete Polls, Oct 25-27: 49-45.
  - Stetson, Oct 26-30, RV: 49-45.
  
  Against Trump's roughly +13 statewide in 2024, that is a 7-9 point D swing. Applied to FL-12's Trump +15.6, it gives about R+7 to R+9 before Bilirakis's incumbency and his roughly 8-to-1 money edge. Kalshi has Moody at 88.5%.
- *Supports the dossier.* The Kalshi ladder for similar Florida seats: FL-21 (R+7, Trump 58.2%) Mast 88.5%; FL-15 (R+9) Lee 93.2%; FL-16 (R+6) Gruters 75.5%. FL-12's 84.5% sits in line with the ladder, perhaps a few points generous to Overman next to FL-21. A headline about a "Mast tied" poll (tcpalm, about Oct 21, via PoliAgg) could not be confirmed. Call the Map shows only Upswing, Sep 21-25: Mast 49-38.
- *Against the dossier.* The 2018 base rate from repo results and lean data: Republican-held seats at a comparable *swing-adjusted* lean flipped about 20-30% of the time with a D+8.6 generic ballot. Many of those had well-funded challengers, which Overman is not, but the base rate argues against confidence much below 10%.

**Bottom line**: the evidence points to roughly R+8 to R+10 and an Overman chance of about 8-12%, against the market's 15.5%. That is too small and too unpolled a gap to call the market clearly wrong. Under the contest scoring, the break-even q for picking Overman is about 0.083, so even the dossier's own 0.08 leaves the two picks close to even in expected score.
