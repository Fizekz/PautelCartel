## Judgment
Poindexter has real advantages: an incumbent hit by a serious scandal, a D+8.5 national environment, a money edge, and Miller nearly out of cash.

Against that, the only October poll is from a D-aligned group and shows just D+4 with Poindexter under 50. Statewide Republicans run about even in the district. Six of ten raters still say Toss-up, and the NYT reports the race tightening and the GOP re-engaging. Wave years do break toss-ups toward the wave party.

I put Poindexter at about 78%, a little below the market's 85%. The gap is not big enough to call the market wrong.
