- Debates were held Oct 8 (Grand Rapids) and Oct 21 (Detroit). Coverage called them combative, with no clear winner. Rogers skipped a proposed Sep 15 debate.
- Trump attacked El-Sayed as a "RADICAL Extremist, Terrorist Sympathizer." Rogers ran ads on El-Sayed's football comments.

- In the Mitchell poll, independents favour El-Sayed 52-38. Rogers consolidates fewer of his own partisans (91%) than El-Sayed does (94%).

## Structure
Statewide plurality. Minor candidates are Green, Libertarian, US Taxpayers and Natural Law. Michigan went Trump +1.4 in 2024. No structural quirks.

## Judgment
The late polling is consistent: a D+4 to D+7 lead among likely voters, from independent and R-labelled pollsters as well as D ones. Quantitative forecasters have moved to Likely D. The main risks are Michigan's history of underestimating Trump-era Republicans (2016, 2020, 2024) and El-Sayed's progressive profile. Those risks justify a 20-25% upset chance, not more. 0.755 is roughly fair to slightly low for El-Sayed.

**Evidence the dossier missed (pro-Rogers caution)**
- In 2024, with the same Republican candidate (Rogers), the final polling averages had Slotkin +3.2: RCP +2.3, 538 +3.6, 270toWin +4.1, DDHQ +2.8. She won by just 0.34 (48.64-48.30). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Senate_election_in_Michigan (fetched Nov 1, 2026)
- Today's D+3.3 average is almost the same as that final 2024 average. A repeat of the 2024 miss would make this a coin flip. This is the strongest specific argument for Rogers.
- Against that, the late high-quality polls (CNN D+7, Mitchell D+7, MSU D+9) run above the average, the trend is toward El-Sayed, and this is a midterm with an unpopular Republican president rather than a presidential year.

**Net:** a 20-28% Rogers chance is defensible, and 0.245 sits inside it. There is no edge in either direction.
