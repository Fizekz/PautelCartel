## Reasoning
- The polling average is Jackson +2 to +3. The national environment is about D+9, with Trump approval about 37%. Most quantitative models have the race at Lean D.
- Collins's 2020 beat of her polls (about 9+ points on the margin) is the main argument that the market overrates Jackson.
- Against that, 2020's poll miss was industry-wide with a D-leaning error. This cycle Republican pollsters (Trafalgar, InsiderAdvantage, Echelon) also show a tie or Jackson ahead, and the midterm environment is far worse for Republicans.
- The market at 68.5% already prices meaningful Collins upside. I put Collins at about 33%. No clear edge.
