## Reasoning
Every poll, including the independent Emerson, has Ager ahead by 3 to 13. Ratings moved one or two steps toward the Democrat during October. He has a large money lead, and the Republican nominee is a late replacement after a scandal, in a D+8 to D+9 national environment.

The main caution is PPP's in-district generic ballot of R+4. That suggests Ager is running well ahead of his party, so a late wave of partisan consolidation could narrow the race.

At 84.5%, the market sits close to my estimate of 86-88% for Ager. I see no specific evidence that the price is wrong.

## Skeptic review
_Independent check, 2026-11-01. Web search was used up after two queries, so this review relies on WebFetch of specific pages, current Wikipedia wikitext pulled by API, and the repo data files._

**Verdict: upheld.** The market (Ager 84.5%) looks about right. My q_underdog for Balkcom is **0.13**.

**Claims verified**
- **RedBox poll checked at the source.** https://afightworthhaving.com/polling/2026/nc-11 (released Oct 25 2026). Field Oct 20-23, n=873 LV, MoE 4.7 including a design effect of 2.0. Text-to-web mode. Ager 49.9, Balkcom 44.1, Groo 0.8, undecided 2.9. Trump approval 45.3/54.0. The same poll has Cooper +5.2 in the district.
- **Emerson / Poll Vault poll.** Oct 12-14, n=388 LV: Ager 56, Balkcom 43. A second aggregator lists it: https://uspollingdata.com/races/house/nc-11/ (race page dated Oct 14). The Poll Vault post itself (https://pollvault.substack.com/p/levee-math) is dated **Oct 22** and is paywalled, so I could not verify the toplines there. Wikipedia cites it as Oct 21.
- **Ratings, Sep 29 vs Nov 1, all confirmed** from the wikitext of revs 1377510186 and 1383160783. Split Ticket was already Lean D on Sep 29, as the dossier said.
- **FEC figures (through Oct 14) match** fec_2026.csv and the Wikipedia table.

**Errors and nuances**
- **The "15:1" money gap is receipts only.** Cash on hand is $620k vs $295k, only about 2:1. Ager has spent $5.25M of his $5.87M, so the end-game candidate cash advantage is much smaller than "15:1" suggests.
- **Emerson poll date.** The source date is Oct 22 per Poll Vault (Oct 21 per Wikipedia), not a firm Oct 22. This does not matter.
- **Emerson's +13 should be discounted.** It is one of 10 Emerson / Poll Vault district polls fielded Oct 12-14, with n=314-417 each, all in polls_2026.csv. That batch leans D across the board: D leads in AZ-02, MO-02, PA-08 and TX-35. The Poll Vault authors' own preview says districts at R+6 or redder "will likely hold." NC-11 is R+5.

**Missed evidence (pro-Ager)**
- **Primary turnout favoured Democrats in a Trump+9.6 seat.** In the March 2026 primaries, 76,084 votes were cast on the D side vs 68,184 on the R side. That is a 52.7% D share against Trump's 45.2% D two-party share (Wikipedia NC page rev 1382921712, election boxes).
- **Statewide environment.** Kalshi's Nov 1 snapshot has Cooper at 0.970 in NC-SEN. RedBox has Cooper +5 inside NC-11, so the top of the ticket helps Ager.
- **Lean D peers.** Across all Lean D House races in the snapshot, the Democrat averages 0.874 on Kalshi (16 races; ratings_2026.csv mean_rating -0.6 to -1.0). NC-11 at 0.845 sits a little below the group but within normal scatter.

**Missed evidence (pro-Balkcom)**
- DDHQ still rates it Toss-up as of Oct 31.
- The PPP in-district generic ballot of R+4 (Sep 10-11) stands.
- No early-vote data was found.

**Net:** There is no actionable mispricing. Ager at about 87% vs the market's 84.5% is within noise.
