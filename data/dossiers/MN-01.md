## Reasoning
Three October polls, one nonpartisan and two D-sponsored, show a tie or a narrow Johnson lead. Trump is at -16 net favorability in a seat he won by 12. Johnson leads in money, and most outside spending is against Finstad. A former Finstad campaign chair crossed over to endorse Johnson. On the other side, the incumbent is rated Lean R by everyone. The statewide Senate average (about D+6 to D+7) suggests the statewide swing since 2024 is modest. A uniform swing would still leave Finstad slightly ahead. I put Johnson at about 0.46, above the market's 0.395. The evidence is specific to this district but comes from polls with small samples, so confidence is limited.

**Factual errors and nuances**
- The dossier says statewide Senate polling is "about D+6 to D+7". The repo shows otherwise: the 13 MN-SEN polls since Sep 28 average about Flanagan +4 to +5. Excluding NYT/Siena (+14) and the latest KSTP/SurveyUSA (+7), they cluster at +3/+4. Against Harris +4.2 in 2024, that is a very small statewide swing, which is a point against a 12-point collapse in a Trump +12 seat. The dossier notes the "modest swing" but understates how small it is.
- HMP's own polling moved from Johnson +1 (GBAO, September) to a tie (PPP, October), not toward Johnson.

**Missed evidence**
- HMP-sponsored PPP polls in comparable Lean R seats show similar ties: NV-02 Flippo +2, Oct 12–13, where the market is 0.605 R. In MN-08 a PPP poll, Oct 25–26, has Stauber +6, and the market prices him at 0.855. D-sponsored district polls in Minnesota are showing far larger swings than the statewide polls, so they are not independent confirmation.
- Finstad's 2024 margin of about 17 (CBS Minnesota, Fox 9 2026-10-22) is about 5 points better than Trump's (R+12.2 two-party, lean_2026.csv). A uniform national swing of about 10 points from his own margin still leaves him around +7, and around +2 from Trump's margin.
- Only one nonpartisan poll exists (SurveyUSA, Johnson +2, MOE ±4.6). That is real district-specific evidence, but it is a single, small sample.

**Net:** Johnson is plausibly a little underpriced: the one nonpartisan poll has him ahead, and he has a money edge. But the statewide swing is small, Finstad overperformed Trump, and HMP-tie polls are routine in seats the market prices at 60–85% R. I would not bank on 0.46. q is about 0.42.
