## Structure
- Trump won the district by about 12 points (lean_2026: R 56.0% two-party). PVI is R+5.
- The race is plurality, with Libertarian Nick Sheedy polling 2-5%.
- The statewide Senate race features independent Seth Bodnar with no strong Democrat, which may scramble turnout.

## Reasoning
- The polling average is about Forstag +2 to +3 excluding the MSU outlier. Note that a D-labelled RedBox poll had Flint +2.
- Forstag leads on money and the raters' momentum is toward him.
- Uniform swing from Trump +12 in a D+9 environment gives about R+1.5.
- A 58% Forstag price fits all of this, so I see no specific edge.
