# CA-07: Doris Matsui (D, inc.) vs Mai Vang (D), a same-party top-two general

**Kalshi (Nov 1):** Matsui 0.635 (0.641 normalised). Vang 0.355. Volume about $93k (thin).
**Verdict:** the market is about right. q(Vang) = 0.35. Confidence: low.

## Polls
No poll fielded after Sep 15 was found.
- Data for Progress, mid-August: Vang 32, Matsui 28, with 40% undecided or not planning to vote. This is the "only public poll," per Drop Site News on Oct 27. https://www.dropsitenews.com/p/aipac-aligned-groups-gift-doris-matsui
- Data for Progress, May 1-5, likely primary voters (n=572): tied at 27 on the initial ballot. Vang led after neutral bios were read (released by the Vang campaign). https://www.maiforus.com/press/may2026poll
- No Matsui-side poll was found.

## Primary (June 2 top-two)
- Vang finished first. As of Jun 9: Vang 30.9%, Republican Zachariah Wooden 22.1% (in third). https://www.abridged.org/?p=16410
- Matsui finished about 2 points behind Vang. One snapshot reports 31.2 vs 29.1-29.2 (unverified against certified results, which I could not retrieve).
- Republicans (Wooden plus Nwobi) together took roughly a quarter of the vote. Those voters now have no candidate of their own.

## Ratings
- Safe D (imputed). Both candidates are Democrats, so no rater rates the intra-party contest.

## Money
- FEC through Oct 14. Matsui raised $7.84M and had $2.0M cash on hand. Vang raised $1.83M and had $0.39M cash on hand.
- Matsui lent her campaign $1.4M for the primary and $4M in total for the general (CalMatters, Oct 23). In Q3 she spent over $1.3M vs about $192k for Vang. https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/10/california-congress-7th-district-race/
- Outside money for Matsui: Pro-Choice Majority Action, $300k of negative ads against Vang (reserved Oct 5). Affordable California, $485k (reserved Oct 7, funded by a $500k gift from the American Foundation for Good Government). Total $785k (Drop Site, Oct 27).
- FEC independent-expenditure aggregates (Nov 1): about $859k supporting Vang, $400k opposing Vang, $140k opposing Matsui.

## News and endorsements
- Matsui is endorsed by Newsom and the California Democratic Party. The California Labor Federation endorsed both candidates.
- Vang is endorsed by the Sacramento Bee editorial board (April). David Hogg campaigned with her. She supports abolishing ICE.
- Vang has faced right-leaning media attacks over her Israel "genocide" statements and over a Pledge of Allegiance incident (May).
- Oct 23 (CalMatters): Matsui (82) is running a mailer attacking Vang's record. Vang is attacking Matsui's donors and self-funding.

## Structure
- New Prop 50 lines. They drop West Sacramento and Natomas and add downtown and southeast Sacramento plus more conservative parts of El Dorado and San Joaquin counties.
- 2024 two-party presidential vote on the new lines: Harris 56.6 / Trump 43.4.
- This is a same-party top-two contest, so Republican and conservative-NPA voters are the swing bloc or will undervote.

## Reasoning
Vang won the primary vote among Democrats and the last public poll had her ahead 32-28. However, about 40% of that sample was undecided, and the undecided pool is likely to be heavily Republican and conservative. Those voters are far more likely to pick the establishment incumbent than an abolish-ICE progressive, or to skip the race. The district is about 43% Trump in 2024. Matsui also has a 4-5:1 spending edge, $785k of late outside negative ads against Vang, and the party apparatus. A rough coalition model, with Vang winning Democrats about 55-60% and Matsui winning participating Republicans about 75%, gives Matsui a modest edge. The 63.5% price is consistent with that. Confidence is low: there is no fall polling, and Republican undervote rates in same-party races are hard to predict.

## Skeptic review
Date: 2026-11-01. Verdict: **weakened**. The "market about right" call ignores an October poll showing a tie, and Matsui's 63.5% looks a little high. Skeptic q_underdog (Vang) = 0.42, against Kalshi 0.355.

**Errors**
- The dossier says "No poll fielded after Sep 15." That is wrong. A Fight Worth Having, a super PAC with ActBlue links, released a RedBox Research poll on 2026-10-25.
  - Fieldwork: Oct 20-23, n=722 LV, MoE ±4.7.
  - Results: Vang 32.2, Matsui 31.5. "Someone else" 18.0 (there is no write-in option in a CA top-two general), undecided 7.9, did not vote in this race 7.0, prefer not to say 3.4.
  - Independents favour Vang 34.6-27.1.
  - https://afightworthhaving.com/polling/2026/ca-07
- The August DFP poll was fielded Aug 18-21 among 651 LV for Working Families Party, Justice Democrats and Leaders We Deserve (Dave Weigel on X; Call the Map). Its sponsors support Vang.
- Self-funding: the Sacramento Bee/Yahoo piece of 2026-10-30 says Matsui has put in "nearly $7 million" of her own money, well above the $4M in the dossier. https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/matsui-definitely-concerned-vang-analysts-002510484.html

**Missed evidence (same Sac Bee piece, Oct 30)**
- Republicans are about 28% of registered voters (SOS, September).
- Matsui's campaign acknowledged polling but declined to release it.
- Six experts expect an extremely close race.
- Christian Grose (USC) says heavy self-funding is "a sign that it's a very tough race for her" and that lower Republican turnout helps Vang.

**Other late developments**
- Axios (2026-10-30) reports that Ro Khanna endorsed Vang. The page returned 403, so I have the headline only. https://axios.com/2026/10/30/ro-khanna-endorse-vang-matsui-jones-gomez-aoc
- State Hornet (late Oct) reports Hasan Piker rallied for Vang.

**Assessment**
The dossier's mechanism, conservative undecideds breaking to Matsui, is plausible. But the October poll already includes those voters: 18% chose "someone else" and 7% skip the race, and Vang still edges ahead.

There are caveats. The pollster is a progressive-leaning PAC's in-house text-to-web unit, and Matsui's late spending of about $7M may not be fully captured. On balance this is close to a coin flip with a slight Matsui lean.
