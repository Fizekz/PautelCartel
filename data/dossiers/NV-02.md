## Structure
Open seat. 2024 lines, unchanged. Plurality vote. Lynn Chapman (Independent American) on the right polled 7–14%, which probably costs Flippo more than it costs Benitez-Thompson. No Democrat has won the seat since it was created in 1981.

## Reasoning
This is a weak, scandal-hit Republican nominee in an R+7 seat during a D+8 to D+9 national environment. The retiring Republican incumbent called him a fraud, and a right-wing third-party candidate takes a share of the conservative vote. These are concrete reasons for an upset. Against that: the only October poll, sponsored by HMP, still had Flippo +2. Benitez-Thompson is badly underfunded. The statewide early vote is weaker for Democrats than in 2022. Raters have not moved past Lean or Tilt R. The market has already fallen from above 70% to 60.5% on these stories. I put Benitez-Thompson at about 0.42, a little above the 0.395 market price, but the gap is not large enough to call the price wrong.

- The dossier attributes the PPP Oct 12–13 poll (Flippo 46/44) to House Majority PAC. uspollingdata lists no sponsor, the cited X post would not load (HTTP 402), and the poll is not on Wikipedia. It is in repo polls_2026.csv flagged as D-partisan, but the HMP attribution is unverified.
- The Nevada Independent debate piece is an opinion column (John L. Smith) and undated. It is not news reporting.

**Missed evidence (R direction)**
- The Freedom Caucus Fund has spent more than $757,000 against Benitez-Thompson. The source is the Nevada Independent, "Billionaires pick sides in the 2nd Congressional District race", undated but mid-to-late October 2026 (it references early voting). That is on top of the more than $1M GOP reservation. https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/billionaires-pick-sides-in-the-2nd-congressional-district-race

**Net:** The scandal and the third-party split are real, but the money is against her, the only October poll (D-partisan) shows Flippo +2, and the statewide early vote is softer for Democrats than in 2022. The market is about right.
