# IA-02: Lindsay James (D) vs Joe Mitchell (R), open R seat (Hinson running for Senate)

**Kalshi Nov 1:** James 0.730 (normalised 0.726), Mitchell 0.275. Underdog: Mitchell.
**Judgement:** market about right. q_underdog (Mitchell) = 0.27. Confidence: medium-low (thin polling).

## Structure
- Plurality vote. The 2024 lines are unchanged. Independent Dave Bushaw and Libertarian Rick Stewart are also on the ballot.
- Lean: R+4. Trump won 55.1% of the two-party vote in 2024 (lean_2026.csv).
- Comparable Iowa seats on Kalshi: IA-01 D (Bohannan) 0.91 and IA-03 D (Trone Garriott) 0.85. IA-02 is the reddest of the three.

## Polls
- Emerson College (Poll Vault), Oct 12-14, 344 LV: James 50, Mitchell 47 (via Wikipedia and DDHQ; I could not confirm it on Emerson's own site).
- I found no other poll since 09-15.
- Older: Global Strategy Group (D, HMP), Jul 14-19, James 46-45. PPP (D, James campaign), Apr 13-14, 675 RV: 36-33.

## Ratings (as of 10-27/30)
- Cook Toss-up, Sabato Lean D (flip), Inside Elections Toss-up.
- Economist, FiftyPlusOne, Silver Bulletin and Split Ticket rate it Lean D. DDHQ, Fox and RCP rate it Toss-up.
- Iowa Factcheck earlier cited Cook at Lean R, so Cook has moved toward D, though I could not date the change.

## Money (FEC through 2026-10-14)
- James raised $3.92M and has $0.27M cash on hand. Mitchell raised $2.92M and has $0.55M cash on hand.
- HMP has spent more than $10M on Iowa House ads (Aug 5 report). The NRCC put Mitchell on its target list.

## News
- 2026-10-01: televised debate (Iowa Press/KTTC).
- NOTUS reported Mitchell's large campaign spending at Mar-a-Lago.
- Hinson's open Senate race (IA-SEN) shares the ballot. The Senate Republicans super PAC has spent $29M there.

## Reasoning
The one nonpartisan October poll has James +3, consistent with an R+4 seat in a D+8-9 generic-ballot environment. Most forecasters rate it Toss-up or Lean D. Mitchell has more cash on hand, but this is not decisive. 73% for James is reasonable, and nothing specific says the market is wrong.

## Skeptic review
_Independent skeptic, 2026-11-01. My web-search budget ran out partway through, so I verified by fetching pages directly._

**Verdict: weakened** (the evidence leans somewhat toward Mitchell compared with the dossier). My q_underdog for Mitchell is **0.35**.

**Claims checked**
- Wikipedia's IA House page (fetched 2026-11-01) confirms the polls:
  - Emerson Oct 12-14, n=344 LV: James 50, Mitchell 47.
  - GSG (D, HMP) Jul 14-19: 46-45.
  - PPP (D, James campaign) Apr 13-14, n=675 RV: 36-33.
- The FEC figures are confirmed.
- Wikipedia's rating dates: Cook Tossup (10-16), Inside Elections Tossup (10-15), Sabato Lean D flip (10-20), DDHQ Tossup (9-25). The later as-of dates in ratings_2026.csv (10-27 to 10-30) show no change since.

**Errors**
- **Debates.** The dossier lists a "2026-10-01 televised Iowa Press debate". Wikipedia lists two debates: Iowa's News Now on Oct 1 and Iowa PBS (the Iowa Press program) on Oct 8. This is minor.

**Missed evidence**
- **Statewide congressional polls point to a closer race than 73%.** Wikipedia IA House page, fetched 2026-11-01:
  - Marist, Oct 8-11, LV (MoE ±6.6): R 50, D 47.
  - co/efficient (R), Oct 15-19, n=863 LV: R 48, D 46.
  - (Marist RV Sep 17-20 had D 50-44.)
  - IA-02 (Trump 55.1% two-party) is only about 1.5 points bluer than the state (about 56.6%). A statewide House vote of R+2 to R+3 therefore implies roughly a tie in IA-02, not James +3 to +5.
- **The IA-SEN race is on the same ballot.** Kalshi has Hinson at 55.5%, and she represents this district now. Her coattails in her home seat are a plausible slight edge for Mitchell.
- **No Democratic absentee surge.** Iowa absentee returns through 10-29 show the D share of returned ballots falling from about 53% (2022) to 47% (2026): https://www.bleedingheartland.com/2026/10/24/iowa-absentee-ballot-numbers-in-the-2026-general-election/ (updated 10-30). The IA-02 tables there are images I could not read.

**Reasoning.** The case for James rests on one small Emerson poll (n=344), an R+4 seat in a D+9 national year, and Sabato's flip call. Statewide Iowa House polling and the absentee data both suggest Iowa is not swinging as far as the national environment. On the other side, every forecaster has it at Tossup or Lean D, and the James +3 poll fits Lean D. I would put James nearer 65% than 73%. That is not a strong enough edge to recommend picking Mitchell.
