## Structure

## Judgment
A Trump +14 seat with a well-funded incumbent faces a challenger with $20K cash on hand. The only poll, Hardy's own, has her down 6, and every major rater says Solid or Safe R. Losing this seat would take a wave larger than 2018. In 2018 a few Trump +10-14 seats did flip (e.g., SC-01, OK-05), but those Democrats had well-funded candidates. A 23.5% price for Hardy looks inflated by thin, late D-leaning flow. I put Hardy at about 10-12%. Kalshi underrates Rouzer.

**Errors and overstatements in the dossier**
- "No race-specific catalyst" is not quite right. Kalshi KXHOUSERACE-NC07-26-D was 0.13 on Oct 17, then 0.15 (Oct 20), 0.19 (Oct 21-22) and 0.22 (Oct 23), on about 13K contracts. That run coincides with the FIRE PAC endorsement listed on Oct 20. It looks like enthusiasm-driven flow, not new fundamentals, which supports the dossier's conclusion. It then sat at 0.22-0.24 through Nov 1 (Kalshi candlesticks API, fetched Nov 1, 2026).
- "A wave larger than 2018" is overstated. Kalshi's own snapshot prices other Trump +11 seats as likely D (OH-07 at D 0.855, Trump +11.2), so the market expects an environment at least like 2018. Even so, a uniform swing of about 10-11 points from Rouzer's 17.2-point 2024 margin still leaves him at about +6. That matches the Hardy-internal R+6 and callthemap's R+8.

**Evidence the dossier missed**
- Peer pricing: Kalshi prices other Solid/Safe R seats at Trump +13-15 in the same range: NC-14 0.225, NE-01 0.225, NY-02 0.175, GA-12 0.145, SC-02 0.125 (races_2026-11-01T074812Z.csv vs lean_2026.csv). NC-07's 0.235 sits at the top of that cluster. The mispricing is real but partly systematic (longshot bias), and it is not a sign that Kalshi traders know something specific about NC-07.
- Against the claim, a Hardy-internal R+6 in August in a Trump +14 seat already implies an 8-point swing toward Democrats. With a Libertarian on the ballot, a 6-8 point district-level polling or modelling miss is not exotic. That is why I put Hardy a little above 10-12%, but still well under 0.235.

**Net:** a Trump +14 seat, an incumbent with an 83-to-1 cash-on-hand edge, Solid/Safe R from Cook, Sabato and Inside, and a 93% model. Hardy is about 0.13.
