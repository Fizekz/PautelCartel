## Skeptic review
_Independent skeptic, 2026-11-01. My web-search budget ran out partway through. The rest of this review uses direct page fetches only._

**Verdict: weakened.** Skeptic q_underdog (Dunlap) = 0.46. The market price is 0.435.

**Claims checked**
- All six polls match Wikipedia (fetched 2026-11-01): Tulchin 10/12-14 D 50-46; Tulchin 9/27-30 D 50-48; UNH 9/17-21 D 51-45 (627 LV, ±3.9, confirmed by Press Herald 2026-09-25); Impact/HMP 9/8-12 49-49; DCCC 8/11-13 R 49-48; co/efficient/NRCC 4/26-28 R 50-40.
- Ratings confirmed on Wikipedia: Cook Lean R (10/9), Inside Lean R (10/15), Sabato Lean R (9/29), DDHQ Toss-up (10/20), Silver Bulletin Toss-up (10/22).
- Boston Globe 2026-10-27 confirms the "Florida Trail" game and the snowbird issue.

**Errors / unverified**
- OnPoint Politics now shows Dunlap at **68%** (updated 2026-10-31), not 57%. Its own *polling average* is **R+0.0**, a tie, so the 68% comes from its fundamentals, not the polls (https://www.onpointpolitics.us/forecast/me/house-2).
- I could not verify "FiftyPlusOne Lean D"; it is not in the Wikipedia ratings table.
- I could not verify that Cook's previous rating was Likely R. Wikipedia shows only Lean R as of 10/9.

**Missed evidence (against the underdog claim)**
- Roll Call 2026-10-31 reports Inside Elections making 63 rating changes toward Democrats, but **ME-02 was not among them**. Inside kept it at Lean R in a wave update. Raters have access to private data, and this holdout is a meaningful signal for LePage.
- UNH's February 2026 poll had LePage 47, Dunlap 46 (Wikipedia). The UNH Survey Center has released no ME-02 poll since 9/24.
- Spectrum News (2026-09-17) reported the Silver Bulletin average at LePage 49.8, Dunlap 47.3. The HMP poll put LePage's favorability at 47 favorable / 49 unfavorable (https://spectrumlocalnews.com/me/maine/politics/2026/09/17/polls-by-national-democrats-show-close-race-in-maine-2nd-congressional-district).
- Five of the six 2026 general-election polls are D internals. Released internals usually lean several points toward their sponsor. Discounting them by about 3 points makes the October picture roughly a tie, which matches OnPoint's R+0.0.

**Assessment.** The race is a genuine coin flip, with the district's structure and LePage's name recognition against a strong D environment. Saying the market underrates Dunlap rests on one independent poll from September and on D internals. A 2-3 point edge is too small and too uncertain to act on.
