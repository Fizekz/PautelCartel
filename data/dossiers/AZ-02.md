## Structure
2024 lines, no change. Plurality vote; Libertarian Goodwin polled at 7% in the summer. Large Navajo/Native electorate, whose midterm turnout is a known risk for Democrats. Crane beat Nez 54.5–45.5 in 2024, an R+2.7 House-vote year.

## Reasoning
The national generic ballot is about D+8 to D+9 (Oct 30 averages). A uniform swing from Crane's 2024 margin of about 9 points would make this roughly even. The two mid-October nonpartisan polls are tied or Nez +2, but both have small samples. On the other side, Crane has a 2:1 advantage in October outside money and in cash, the Libertarian is on the ballot, Native midterm turnout tends to fall off, and HMP has reportedly pulled back. Raters still say Lean R. The market's 40.5% for Nez fits the market's general wave pricing for Lean R seats (about 60% R). I see no specific reason to move it much. I put Nez at 0.42.
