## Structure
New 2025 Texas map. The district covers Hispanic-heavy east and southeast Harris County plus Liberty County. Trump +20.1 in 2024; Cook PVI R+9. Plurality vote, two candidates. The seat number previously belonged to Democrat Al Green.

## Judgment
The only independent poll has Mealer +6. The two Democratic polls are R+6 and R+1. Gutierrez is badly underfunded, and the GOP has a large outside-spending edge. On the other side, Trump's weak approval and a Latino swing back toward Democrats (Texas statewide races competitive) give a real upset path, and the market drifted from 0.84 to 0.76 in the final week. The model (84%) is slightly more bullish on Mealer than Kalshi. I see no specific evidence that Kalshi is wrong in either direction.
