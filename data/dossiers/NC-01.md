## Judgment
The national environment is about D+8.5 on the generic ballot (repo national_2026.csv, Oct 30). Shifting the district's Trump+11.6 by roughly that swing gives about even, and Davis has a record of running ahead of the top of the ticket. The poll average of about D+3 agrees with that.

The case for Buckhout: the October polls tightened, and the D-aligned RedBox poll has Davis up only 1.8, about 4 points behind Cooper. Two independent models put Davis at 55-73%.

The case for Davis: every rating move this month went his way, and in wave years Lean D incumbents historically win at very high rates. Outside money is even.

Overall, the market's 84.5% looks a few points rich but not clearly wrong. I put Buckhout at about 21%.
