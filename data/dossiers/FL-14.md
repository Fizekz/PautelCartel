# FL-14: Rep. Kathy Castor (D, inc.) vs Mike Beltran (R), redrawn 2026 map

**Kalshi Nov 1:** Castor 0.735 (normalised 0.742), Beltran 0.255. Underdog: Beltran.
**Judgement:** market about right. q_underdog (Beltran) = 0.30. Confidence: medium-low.

## Structure
- New 2026 Florida map. Trump would have won the new FL-14 54.7-44.2 (Ballotpedia via Florida Press, 10-20). Under the old lines Harris won 53.3-45.6.
- The map dropped Democratic parts of St. Petersburg and added rural east Hillsborough.
- The electorate is about 52% white, 28% Hispanic and 12% Black.
- Libertarian Brian Lambert and two write-ins are also on the ballot.
- Cook PVI R+4.

- RedBox Research (A Fight Worth Having), Oct 20-23, 708 LV: Castor 45.6, Beltran 44.7 (via DDHQ; sponsor leaning unclear).

## Ratings
- Cook moved from Lean R (around Aug 18) to Toss-up. Inside Elections went from Tilt R (September) to Tilt D (as of 10-29), a move toward D within the last month or so. Sabato is Toss-up (10-27).
- DDHQ, Economist, FiftyPlusOne, Fox and Split Ticket rate it Lean D. Silver Bulletin rates it Likely D, RCP Toss-up.
- DDHQ's model gives Castor 69%.

## Money (FEC through 2026-10-14)
- Castor raised $4.93M and has $0.58M cash on hand. Beltran raised $2.73M and has $1.73M cash on hand; about $2M of his total is self-loans.
- Outside spending: Trump's midterm PAC put nearly $5M into the race (Tampa Bay Times via Florida Press, 10-20), and CLF announced $6.5M for Tampa ads. On the Democratic side, the DCCC has Castor in its Frontline program and is running ads on Beltran's insurance-industry ties, and EDF Action has an anti-Beltran ad.

## News
- 2026-09-01: Trump endorsed Beltran.
- 2026-10-02: WMNF radio debate. 2026-10-16: Tiger Bay forum.

## Reasoning
Castor is a 10-term incumbent with a personal vote in a district now about Trump +10. The environment is D+8-9 nationally, and the polls show Castor +1 to +5. Florida has shifted right faster than the nation, and Florida polls overestimated Democrats in 2018-2024 (background, not re-sourced). Republicans have heavy outside money (about $11.5M announced from Trump's PAC and CLF), and Beltran had more cash on hand on 10-14. These points balance Castor's incumbency. 74% looks right to slightly generous; I put Beltran at 0.30. That is not enough of an edge to call an upset.

_Independent skeptic, 2026-11-01. My web-search budget ran out partway through, so I verified by fetching pages directly._

**Verdict: upheld** (market_about_right). My q_underdog for Beltran is **0.31**.

**Claims checked**

  - St. Pete Polls July, n=469 LV: 46.2-37.5.
  - Cook moved from Lean R (Aug 18) to Toss-up.

**Errors**
- The Florida Press article does not mention the CLF $6.5M figure. Its source is not shown in the dossier, so treat it as unverified.
- The Inside Elections Tilt D rating comes from ratings_2026.csv (10-29). The Florida Press article stops at Tilt R.

**Missed evidence**
- **Statewide generic ballot favours Republicans** (Wikipedia FL House page, fetched 2026-11-01):
  - NYT/Siena, Oct 19-25, n=813 LV: R 47, D 42.
  - Univision/YouGov, Oct 2-15, n=825 LV: R 49, D 44.
  - FL-14 is about 1.3 points bluer than the state (two-party). R+5 statewide therefore implies about R+2 to R+3 in FL-14 before Castor's incumbency, which argues for a closer race.
- **Statewide early and mail vote favours Democrats compared with 2022** (WUSF 2026-10-30, data through 10-29): https://www.wusf.org/politics-issues/2026-10-30/nearly-4-million-florida-votes-have-been-cast-for-the-midterm-election
  - 2026: R about 1.57M vs D about 1.51M, a net R lead of about 60k.
  - 2022: R led early in-person by about 500k and D led mail by about 200k, a net R lead of about 300k.
  - That is a much better D showing than 2022, despite Republicans' larger registration edge since. There are no Hillsborough-specific figures.

**Reasoning.** Two signals offset each other: the statewide R+5 polls point toward Beltran, and the early vote points toward Castor. Castor's 10-term incumbency and the October ratings drift toward D support her. About 70% for Castor is fair, and I see no specific mispricing.
