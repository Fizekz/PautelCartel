## Judgment
Brown leads by 3-4 in a deep set of polls, including R-sponsored InsiderAdvantage, Trafalgar and Big Data. He has a D+8 to D+9 national environment and Tilt or Lean D ratings. The main counterweight is that Ohio polls have overstated Democrats in 2018 (Brown, DeWine-Cordray), 2020, 2022 and 2024 (Brown lost by 3.6 after leading in many polls), and Husted has a large outside-money edge. A +3.5 lead with a typical Ohio correction of about 3 is close to a coin flip on polls alone, but the environment pushes Brown back up. The market's 0.71 is already a discount from what an unadjusted +3.5 lead implies (about 0.75-0.80), so it appears to price the Ohio polling history. There is no specific new evidence that it is wrong. q(Husted) = 0.32.

## Skeptic review
_Independent skeptic, 2026-11-01. The web-search budget was exhausted, so this review relies on direct fetches._

**Verdict: WEAKENED (market_about_right).** The evidence leans further toward Husted than the dossier says. Skeptic q(Husted) = 0.36, against Kalshi's 0.295.

**Errors**
- "R-sponsored ones (InsiderAdvantage, Trafalgar, Big Data)": no Trafalgar Ohio Senate poll appears in polls_2026.csv. Big Data's poll is from Sep 26-27, so it is stale.
- "Brown leads by 3-4": the Oct 31 aggregates are smaller. Average Brown +2.6, made up of 270toWin +2.0, DDHQ +2.4, FiftyPlusOne +2.9, RCP +2.6 and Silver Bulletin +3.0. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Ohio (fetched 2026-11-01).
- ActiVote's Oct 31 release (50.2-49.8) was fielded Oct 18-31, with a median date of Oct 24 and n=400 LV. Source: activote.net.

**Missed evidence**
- FiftyPlusOne is listed at Toss-up as of Oct 31 on Wikipedia. ratings_2026.csv (Oct 30) has it at Lean D, which suggests a late move toward R. Fox (Oct 27), Silver Bulletin (Oct 28) and Split Ticket (Oct 27) remain Lean D; Cook, Sabato, DDHQ, NBC, RCP and the Economist are Toss-up.
- The Kalshi price rose from 68% on Oct 27 to 71.5% on Nov 1, while the poll averages did not widen. Kalshi News (2026-10-27) quotes Prof. David Niven (University of Cincinnati) expecting the Democratic early-vote lead to shrink on Election Day.
- I could not verify the AdImpact $198.5M vs $94.7M figures because NBC4 returned 403 to the fetch.

**Assessment.** A lead of about 2.6 in a state where polls overstated Democrats in each of the last four cycles (per the dossier) is closer to 60-65% than 71.5%, even allowing for the D+8-9 national environment. The edge is modest, but it runs toward Husted.
