## Structure
This is the first election on Ohio's new 2025 commission map. The district covers downtown Columbus, southern and western Franklin County, and parts of Pickaway and Miami counties. Trump won it by about 10 in 2024 on the new lines. Libertarian Barrington is on the ballot.

## Judgment
On uniform swing (about D+8-9 nationally), a Trump+10 seat is roughly a toss-up before incumbency. Democrats are spending real money, about $4M, and their tracking moved from Carey +5 to Leonard +3. However, all the polls are Democratic-sponsored and released by the spender. Lean R incumbents usually hold even in waves. The two effects roughly offset, so the market's 62.5% for Carey looks fair to slightly low. I do not have specific evidence that it is wrong.
