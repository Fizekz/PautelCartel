Missed evidence and caveats:
- Davis has the post-primary backing of Reproductive Freedom for All (Wikipedia).
- The Hart trend from +4 to a tie looks like late movement, but in a campaign-released series the sponsor chooses when to publish. A tie released three days before the election is a standard closing-message release. Davis's 3:1 late cash edge is real, but in absolute terms ($1.25M) it is small for a statewide race.
- Fundamentals: Trump took 61.2% of the two-party vote (lean_2026.csv), about R+22 against a national R+1.6. A uniform swing to national D+9 gives about Letlow +12, and Louisiana is inelastic. After a 4-5 point discount on the internal, polls point to about Letlow +4-5. A blend of polls and fundamentals gives about Letlow +8. With an SD of about 6, that is a 9-11% Davis chance.

Assessment: the only evidence for an upset is a sponsor-released internal. That is not enough to call Kalshi's 10.5% too low.
