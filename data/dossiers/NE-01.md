## News
- The only debate was on Sep 24. Flood says the rating changes reflect the national environment, not polling (WOWT, 2026-10-27).
- Independent Austin Ahlman withdrew, so Backemeyer has a clean two-way race apart from the Libertarian and America First candidates.

## Structure
- Plurality voting, and the district lines did not change. Trump won it by 13 points in 2024 (2-party share 56.6/43.4), and Cook PVI is R+6. Flood won his last two races by 20 and 16 points. The district includes Lincoln (Lancaster County). The generic-ballot average is about D+8 to D+9 (Wikipedia aggregate, 2026-10-30).

## Reasoning
A uniform swing from 2024 to a D+9 national environment puts the district at about R+3, before counting Flood's incumbency. The only recent poll is a Democratic internal showing Flood +1. Democratic internals usually overstate Democrats, so the true margin is probably Flood +3 to +5. HMP's late $1M buy and the NRCC's defensive spending show both parties think the race is in play. That fits a 20-30% upset chance, which is about where Kalshi is (23%). I see no specific evidence for moving away from the market.
