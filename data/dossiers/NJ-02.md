## Structure
Plurality race on unchanged lines. Trump won the district 56.4-43.6 two-party in 2024 (lean_2026.csv; Cook PVI R+5). Van Drew is a four-term incumbent who switched parties in 2019.

## Judgment
With a D+8 to D+9 environment applied to a Trump +13 seat, a generic open race would be about R+3 to R+4. Van Drew adds incumbency and a 2.5:1 money edge. The Democrats' own October poll has him up 10 (8 informed). Every major rater has it at Likely R; Likely R seats historically go to the favoured party about 85-90% of the time, and Lilly's model is at 89%. The late $1.5M Project 218 buy and Inside's Solid-to-Likely shift show some Democratic upside, but not enough for 30%. The thin market seems to be pricing generic wave risk. I set q(Mullock) = 0.20.
