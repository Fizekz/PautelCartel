## Structure
Plurality race with no runoff in the general. Libertarian Ted Brown is on the ballot. Texas has no party registration, so partisan readings of the early vote are modelled estimates. The Senate race is not affected by the new TX House map.

## Judgment
Talarico leads by about 3 in a large, consistent body of polls, including Republican-sponsored ones. He also has a huge money edge in candidate funds, though Musk's super PAC money offsets it, along with favourable ratings moves and a D+8 to D+9 national generic ballot. Against that, Texas polls underestimated Republicans in 2020, 2022 and 2024, and the early electorate is still Trump +8 by 2024 vote. A 3-point lead with typical state polling error (sd about 4-5) maps to roughly 70-75%. A Texas-specific correction pulls that to about 68%. Kalshi's 0.69 sits right there, so there is no specific evidence the market is wrong. q(Paxton) = 0.32.
