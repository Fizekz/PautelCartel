## Reasoning
Both public polls in this window show Shah up 2 to 4. Neither is from an independent gold-standard pollster, and one comes from a Democratic firm. Fundamentals point clearly to Shah: an R+1 open seat in a D+8 to D+9 generic-ballot environment. Ratings moved toward him, and the models (Economist, Split Ticket, Silver, DDHQ) are at Likely D. Feely's celebrity and Sinema's backing are real but small factors. A ~4-point expected margin with district-level error gives roughly 75–80% for Shah, consistent with the market. I see no specific evidence of mispricing.
