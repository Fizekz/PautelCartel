## News
- The Alaska Democratic Party, DCCC Red to Blue and Blue Dog PAC endorsed Hill after the primary, and the other Democrats withdrew. Hafner, a federal prisoner and perennial candidate, is still on the ballot. JD Vance rallied in Fairbanks on Oct 6 and Trump in Wasilla on Oct 13. The Anchorage Daily News endorsed Begich.
- The NRCC claims Hill's "progressive" label rose from 22% to 52%.

## Structure
- Ranked-choice general with four candidates: Begich, Hill, Hafner (D) and McDermott (L). Hafner's 5-9% should mostly transfer to Hill; some ballots will exhaust. Begich led the August top-four primary 44.6-32.5. The PVI is R+6.

## Reasoning
Hill has gained since September. The nonpartisan polls (NYT/Siena, ASR) and the Republican polls give Begich a 6-8 point final-round lead, while the Democratic polls show the race at roughly even to Begich +2. A sensible average is about Begich +4 to +5. Alaska polls are noisy, and the national environment and Peltola's Senate lead help Hill. On the other side, Begich has far more money and Republican groups have attacked Hill late. A roughly 25% chance for Hill fits that picture, and Kalshi has him at 22%. The gap is too small to call the market wrong.
