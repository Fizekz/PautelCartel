## Reasoning
The generic ballot is about D+8 to D+9 (aggregates on 2026-10-30). A uniform swing from Bean's +14.6 in 2024 would leave him at roughly +4 to +6. That is closer than the Solid/Safe ratings suggest, and it is the best case for Holloway's ~20%. Against that:
- Holloway has no money for paid media. She has lost twice before.
- Most models (Split Ticket Safe, Economist, Silver and FPO Likely) put Bean above 85%. Only DDHQ is at Lean R.
- There is no D poll or D spending suggesting a real contest.

My estimate is about 13% for Holloway, so the market overprices her slightly. Bean is the side to take.

**Claims checked**
- The rating moves are correct. On the ratings page, Split Ticket went Likely R -> Safe R and DDHQ went Likely R -> Lean R. The FL-04 district page dates DDHQ's Lean R to Oct 30, 2026. All other ratings are unchanged as listed.
- FEC through Oct 14 is correct: Bean $2,682,267 raised and $577,964 cash on hand; Holloway $58,546 raised and $44,105 cash on hand (Wikipedia FL House page, FEC table).
- The primary is correct: Holloway won the Aug 18 primary with 57.3% (Wikipedia FL-04 results box). The 2024 result is correct: Bean 222,364 to Holloway 165,912, 57.3-42.7 two-party (data/clean/results_house.csv).
- The ballot is correct: Mike Sell (Forward) and Todd Schaefer (IND) are on it (Wikipedia).
- The UNF poll is from 2024 and correctly labelled as such. It is not evidence about 2026.

**Errors:** None material. One soft spot: the "+4 to +6 under uniform national swing" baseline uses the national swing. Florida is swinging much less than the nation (see below), so that baseline is too generous to Holloway.

**Missed evidence (supports Bean)**
- Florida statewide polls show a much smaller D swing than the D+8 to D+9 national generic ballot (national figure: data/clean/national_2026.md, aggregates of 2026-10-30). FL-SEN polls, all from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_special_election_in_Florida?oldid=1383159994:
  - NYT/Siena, Oct 19-25, 813 LV: Moody 50, Nixon 44.
  - UNF, Oct 12-19, 1,471 LV: Moody 49, Nixon 45.
  - Stetson, Oct 26-30, 852 RV: Moody 49, Nixon 45.
  - Kalshi has Moody at 0.885 (same snapshot).
- FL-04's 2024 vote was close to the state's: Trump +12.0 two-party in FL-04 (lean_2026.csv), against Trump 56.09-42.99 (+13.1) statewide (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election_in_Florida).
- Bean ran about 2.6 points ahead of Trump in 2024.
- Putting these together, a Florida-specific swing (about 7 points) applied to Bean's +14.6 gives about Bean +7 to +8. With a district error of about 6 points, that is roughly a 10-13% chance for Holloway, before counting her 45:1 money deficit.

**Counter-evidence:** DDHQ moved toward Holloway (Likely -> Lean R) on Oct 30. That is the only late quantitative move in her direction. The market may also be pricing a wave correlation across Florida seats. Neither is enough to justify 19.5%.

**Bottom line:** The direction is right, and FL statewide polling supports it more strongly than the dossier argued. The price gap (0.195 vs about 0.13) is modest.
