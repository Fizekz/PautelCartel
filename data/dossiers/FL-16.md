## News
- Gruters is Trump-endorsed. She is married to RNC chair and state senator Joe Gruters, and is backed by several local sheriffs.
- Kirschner is a former Sarasota mayor. The Bradenton Times endorsed him. Former Manatee GOP commission chair George Kruse publicly backed Kirschner (Kotok Report, Oct 11).
- Florida Phoenix (Sep 25) called Gruters the early favourite and said the race was "getting increasingly interesting."
- NPA candidate Mark Davis is also on the ballot.
- I found no scandal or withdrawal. I had no Florida early-vote party breakdown for the district; my search budget ran out.

## Structure
Redrawn in the May 2026 Florida mid-decade map. The district now covers all of Manatee, DeSoto and Hardee counties plus parts of Sarasota, Pinellas and Polk. Cook PVI R+6; Trump 56.9% two-party in 2024. Plurality vote, three candidates.

## Judgment
The only polls are Democratic internals, and the cleaner ballot test in them still has Gruters +4. Statewide Florida polling implies a mid-single-digit R margin. The money and outside-spending edge is Republican, and an independent model puts Gruters at 77%. Kalshi's 0.755 matches that. Upside for Kirschner comes from the national D environment and the late-October D drift in the market, but no specific evidence pushes his chance above about 22-25%.

**Corrections**
- Callthemap attributes the PPP poll to the **American Democracy Project**. The same group spent **$103K against Gruters**, so "no D outside money" is not quite right, though the amount is small. The $1.7M figure is "for or against Gruters": about $1.5M supporting her (Conservatives for American Excellence and Defend American Jobs) plus that $103K against. Source: callthemap page above.
- George Kruse is a Republican and was 2025 chairman of the Manatee County Board of Commissioners. He is not a "GOP commission chair" (Kotok, Oct 11).
- The Stetson statewide poll is cited only to the politicalwire.com homepage. I could not verify it.
