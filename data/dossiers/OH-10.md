# OH-10: Rep. Mike Turner (R) vs Kristina Knickerbocker (D)

**Kalshi, Nov 1:** Turner (REP) 0.685, Knickerbocker (DEM) 0.315. Event volume is about $245k.
**Judgement:** market about right. q(Knickerbocker) = 0.34. Confidence: medium-low.

## Structure
- **New lines:** Ohio adopted a commission map on Oct 31, 2025. The new district takes in all of Montgomery and Greene counties and part of Butler.
- **Partisan lean:** 2024 presidential vote on the new lines was Trump 53.9 / Harris 46.1 two-party (about Trump +8). PVI is R+4.
- **Incumbent:** Turner is a 12-term incumbent and has won every race by 10+ points; he won 2024 59.5/40.5 two-party on the old lines.
- **Third candidate:** Libertarian Thomas McMasters, former Huber Heights mayor. The race is plurality.

## Polls
- **DCCC internal, Oct 12-13, 535 LV:** Knickerbocker 47, Turner 44, McMasters 4. Turner's job approval was 36/41 (DCCC memo, Oct 20, https://dccc.org/dccc-polling-memo-knickerbocker-leads-turner-by-3-in-oh-10/).
- **Global Strategy Group (Knickerbocker campaign), Jul 20-23, 700 LV:** Turner 49, Knickerbocker 42. With McMasters included: Turner 45, Knickerbocker 40, McMasters 4.
- **FM3 for House Majority PAC, Jul 6-12, 535 LV:** Turner 48, Knickerbocker 41.
- No independent poll exists, per Medill on the Hill (Oct 21) and the Ohio Capital Journal (Oct 28).
- For context, the Ohio Senate race polls at Brown (D) +3 to +4 on average in late October (Suffolk Oct 26: 47-44), against Trump +11 in Ohio in 2024. That is a very large statewide swing.

## Ratings
- **Cook:** moved Likely R to Lean R on Oct 16, citing "private polling now shows Turner neck-and-neck" (DCCC excerpt, https://dccc.org/%F0%9F%9A%A8-ratings-change-cook-political-report-shifts-oh-10-in-democrats-direction-2/).
- **Sabato and Inside Elections:** Lean R.
- **Others:** FiftyPlusOne Toss-up. RCP, Silver Bulletin and Split Ticket Lean R. DDHQ, Economist and Fox Likely R.

## Money
- **Candidate FEC:** Turner raised about $1.37M and Knickerbocker about $1.03M; Knickerbocker outraised Turner in Q3.
- **Outside spending (OpenSecrets via Dayton Daily News, Oct 28):** $9.11M across 19 committees, about 63.6% from liberal groups.
  - House Majority PAC: $3.36M.
  - Safety & Affordability PAC: $2.11M.
  - Your Community PAC (D dark money): $1.83M.
  - Spending against Turner: $4.67M. Spending for Turner: $0.64M.
  - Pro-Turner super PACs are mainly MAGA Inc.-funded. The NRCC has also aired ads for Turner.
- A GOP tracker counts $6.5M on Dayton TV and radio, $4.32M of it from Democrats.

## News
- Medill reports Turner is not on the NRCC's vulnerable-incumbent list (Oct 21).
- The Ohio Capital Journal reports Ohio Democrats outraised Republicans in the home stretch (Oct 28).

## Reasoning
- A uniform national swing of about 10 points puts the new seat around D+2 on the presidential baseline. Ohio statewide polls imply an even larger swing.
- Turner's personal overperformance, historically about 10+ points, should keep him slightly ahead.
- The only October poll is a D internal at Knickerbocker +3. Adjusted for sponsor bias, that is roughly a tie to Turner +2. Cook calls it neck-and-neck on private polling.
- Democrats are out-spending Republicans heavily on outside money, and HMP has not pulled out (unlike FL-07).
- I put Knickerbocker at about 34-35%, close to the market's 31.5%. Not enough to call the market wrong.
