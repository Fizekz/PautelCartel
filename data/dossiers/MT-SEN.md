Errors and caveats:
1. **The "Alme +10.5" average is inflated by stale aggregates.** Wikipedia's mean includes 270toWin (Alme +18.0, data through Oct 18) and RCP (+12.0, through Oct 18). The three aggregates updated Oct 30 with data through Oct 23 show Alme +6.2 (DDHQ), +8.0 (FiftyPlusOne) and +8.4 (Silver Bulletin). The current lead is about 6-8 points, not 10.5.
2. **The MSU Billings poll is not a likely-voter poll and is weak as the anchor for "most neutral."** The PDF (https://www.msubillings.edu/class/nams/polls/2026-mountain-states-poll-report.pdf, Oct 2026, extracted 2026-11-01) describes 720 responses: 591 from an online quota panel (Centiment) and 129 by RDD phone, fielded Oct 5-21 and "weighted according to Montana census data." It describes no likely-voter screen, so Wikipedia's "adults" label is right and Political Wire's "LV" (2026-10-29) is not supported. Only 74% of Republicans back Alme in it, and 17% are undecided. Its crosstabs also show Bodnar leading among voters 70+ (45 to 28) and 60-69 (32 to 46 Alme). That is noisy, but high-turnout older voters are not clearly Alme's.
3. The dossier says "Wikipedia says adults, Political Wire says LV." The primary source settles this: census-weighted, with no LV screen.

Missed evidence:
- Rutgers-Eagleton (Montana Free Press), Aug 27-Sep 7, 845 RV: Alme 38, Bodnar 30, Bankhead 16, Austin 5 (Wikipedia). MTPR on 2026-10-14 cites it as an "eight-point" Alme lead.
- Bankhead has only $22.8K cash on hand (FEC, Oct 14, via Wikipedia). She cannot fund a late push, so further erosion of her share toward Bodnar is plausible. Concord shows her falling from 13 to 10 between Oct 6-8 and Oct 22-23.
- Joe Manchin and Adam Kinzinger are also listed as Bodnar endorsers (Wikipedia). The Montana Libertarian Party has disavowed Austin.

Assessment: the core logic holds. Bankhead's 10-20% splits the anti-Alme vote, and no nonpartisan likely-voter poll shows Bodnar ahead. Still, an Alme lead of about 7 points in the fresh aggregates, with 10-15% undecided in a four-way race with an independent (where polling error is large), puts Bodnar nearer 13-16% than 11%. Independents like Osborn (NE 2024) tend to be squeezed late, which argues against going higher. This is not a confident underdog play.
