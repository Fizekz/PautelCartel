**Verdict:** market about right. q_underdog (Alvarado) = 0.43. Confidence: low-medium.

- Oct 22: Kentucky Democratic Party mailers promote independent Jay Bowman to siphon R votes (https://spectrumnews1.com/ky/louisville/news/2026/10/22/6th-district-ads-independent). Debates were held Oct 21-22 and Oct 27-28.

## Structure
2024 lines. Trump 57.7% 2p (~+15), Cook PVI R+7. Independent Jay Bowman and a Kentucky Party candidate are also on the ballot. Charles Booker (D) leads the Senate ticket.

## Judgement
Every poll since late September has Dembo ahead by 3-9, but all are D-sponsored. Republicans have released no counter-poll, which is mildly informative. A fundamentals model (R+15 seat, D+8.5 nation) still says lean R, which pulls the other way. Shading the D polls by ~4 points leaves Dembo about +2. The ~55% price is fair; my own estimate is Dembo ~0.57.

## Skeptic review (2026-11-01)
**Verdict: upheld** (market about right). Skeptic q_underdog (Alvarado) = 0.45.

Caveat: WebSearch budget exhausted; I verified by direct WebFetch, local files and the Kalshi public API only. Kentucky Lantern and fox56 both returned HTTP 403, so the $6.2M PAC breakdown and the Change Research article are unverified by me. Their poll numbers do match data/clean/polls_2026.csv.

**What I checked, and what held up**
- All five Sep-Oct D-sponsored polls match data/clean/polls_2026.csv: PPP/HMP 47-41; DCCC 46-40; Change 47-38; Lake 47-40 (48-45 head to head); GQR 42-37 (46-43 head to head).
- The earlier polls also match and show the trend. GQR (Jun) had Alvarado 42-39, and DCCC (Jul) had a 47-47 tie. Dembo's lead built in late Sep-Oct.
- Inside Elections Tilt R -> Toss-up: confirmed (Roll Call, Oct 31).
- KY Democratic Party mailers boosting independent Jay Bowman: confirmed, https://spectrumnews1.com/ky/louisville/news/2026/10/22/6th-district-ads-independent (Oct 22). Bowman says he had nothing to do with them. More than 70,000 voters (~13%) are registered outside the two major parties.
- FEC figures match data/clean/fec_2026.csv.

**Errors**
- Event dates in the dossier are off by a day. Beshear's rally with Dembo in Lexington was Thu Oct 29, not Oct 30. Emmer and Barr were in Jessamine County on Wed Oct 28. The Kentucky Lantern/Yahoo story ran Oct 30. https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/state-race-dembo-alvarado-final-093038950.html

**Evidence the dossier missed**
- The dossier gave only the anti-candidate totals for the final week. The full Oct 29 split was: anti-Dembo (No Going Back PAC) $650k+; pro-Dembo $320k+; anti-Alvarado $1M+; pro-Alvarado $150k+. That puts Democratic-side outside money at about $1.3M against about $0.8M for Republicans. (Kentucky Lantern via Yahoo, Oct 30)
- Emmer did not cite any GOP numbers. He offered a landline/text-message argument for why the polls are "not right." Alvarado claimed he is "surging" and that Dembo has "peaked," also with no data. (same source)
- Kalshi price path (KXHOUSERACE-KY06-26-D daily close): 0.25 in late Sep; 0.31-0.33 through Oct 21; 0.39 on Oct 22; 0.45 on Oct 23 (after the PPP release); 0.47 on Oct 24-28; 0.49 on Oct 29; 0.56 on Oct 30; 0.555 on Nov 1. The market is thin, with roughly $2k a day of volume, so the price is less informative than in the larger races.

**Assessment**
Five D polls from four different firms agree on Dembo +3 to +9. No Republican has released a counter-poll, and that silence is informative. The Dem-side final-week money edge and the Bowman mailers both point the same way.

The fundamentals still lean R, at roughly R+5 in a D+8 environment. Dembo at 0.55-0.57 is fair, and I find no evidence the market is wrong in either direction.
