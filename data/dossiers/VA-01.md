## Structure
The 2024 map is still in use. Virginia's April 21, 2026 redistricting referendum was voided by the Virginia Supreme Court on May 8 (data/clean/redistricting_2026.csv). Trump won the district by 5 in 2024.

## Judgment
This is a genuine toss-up. The best independent poll has Taylor +4, but Democrats' own late polls show only +1. Inside Elections (Tilt R) and DDHQ (Lean R) give Wittman a slight edge, and Cook and Sabato have it as a toss-up. In wave years toss-ups lean toward the out-party, which supports Taylor around 60%. I put Taylor at about 60% versus the market's 63%. That gap is too small and too noisy to call the price wrong.
