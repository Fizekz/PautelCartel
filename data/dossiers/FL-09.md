- A trial court declined to block the map. Soto calls it unconstitutional.

## Structure
- New 2026 Florida map. The district now covers Osceola plus parts of Orange and Polk, Indian River, Highlands, Okeechobee and Glades.
- 2024 two-party presidential vote on the new lines: Trump 59.0 / Harris 41.0 (PVI R+8). Soto is effectively running in mostly new territory, so his incumbency is worth less than usual.
- Plurality rules, two candidates.

## Reasoning
Fundamentals favour Green. Uniform swing from Trump +18 under a D+8.5 environment still leaves Green ahead by about 8 points. On the other side, the probabilistic models (DDHQ, Economist, Split Ticket) and RCP have it as a toss-up, and two ratings moved toward Soto in the last week. The district's Puerto Rican and Hispanic voters may swing more than uniformly, given the 2025 Hispanic snap-back. The only polls are Soto internals showing a tie, which after sponsor lean points to Green up a few. Florida's statewide early vote was roughly even by party through Oct 24, which is not decisive. The 63.5% price for Green sits between the fundamentals (about 70% or more for Green) and the models (about 50%). I see no specific evidence to call it wrong.
