# NY-21: Anthony Constantino (R) vs Blake Gendebien (D), open seat

**Kalshi, Nov 1:** Constantino (REP) 0.675, Gendebien (DEM) 0.325. Event volume is about $214k.
**Judgement:** market about right, with Gendebien slightly underpriced. q(Gendebien) = 0.36. Confidence: medium-low.

## Structure
- The seat is open; Stefanik is not running. New York kept its 2024 map.
- 2024 presidential vote was Trump 60.4 / Harris 39.6 two-party (about Trump +21). PVI is R+10.
- Independent Christopher Schmidt is running as a write-in and is not on the ballot. Constantino's campaign objected to Siena including him (WCAX, Oct 12).
- The race is plurality.

## Polls (all since Aug; none after Oct 13 found)
- **Siena (Times Union/WPTZ/WAMC), Sep 30-Oct 6, 496 LV:** Gendebien 46, Constantino 42. Constantino's favourability was 39/52, and he won only about 65% of Republicans (WCAX, Oct 12; Daily Gazette, Oct 29).
- **PPP for House Majority PAC (D), Oct 12-13, 613 LV:** Constantino 46, Gendebien 44.
- **Impact Research (Gendebien internal), Sep 29-Oct 4, 500 LV:** Constantino 48, Gendebien 45.
- **PPP for The Bench (D), Sep 9-10, 557 RV:** Constantino 48, Gendebien 43.
- **Impact Research (Gendebien internal), Aug 30-Sep 2:** Constantino 47, Gendebien 44.
- **J.L. Partners, Aug 15-17, 500 LV:** Constantino 50, Gendebien 40 (https://jlpartners.com/new-york-21st-district-likely-voter-polling).
- **SurveyUSA/WNYT, Aug 4-8, 612 LV:** Gendebien 43, Constantino 39, Schmidt 5.

## Ratings
- **Cook, Sabato and Inside Elections:** all Lean R (Oct 27-30).
- **Split Ticket:** Toss-up. Economist, RCP and Silver Bulletin: Lean R. DDHQ, FiftyPlusOne and Fox: Likely R.
- I found no dated rating change in the last month.

## Money
- **Constantino:** self-funded. He loaned his campaign $17M and accepts no contributions. FEC receipts are $14.46M, with $2.29M cash on hand on Oct 14.
- **Gendebien:** $7.66M FEC receipts and $364k cash on hand on Oct 14. He raised $471k from Oct 1-14 and reported $73.5k in 48-hour reports from Oct 17-20 (Yahoo/Daily Gazette, Oct 27).
- Trump's Save American Freedom PAC is running TV and digital ads against Gendebien; the amount was not given.
- CatholicVote Common Good endorsed Gendebien on Oct 21 and is helping with his get-out-the-vote effort.

## News
- **No debate:** Gendebien skipped the CBS6 debate in early October and Constantino debated an empty podium (Daily Gazette).
- **GOP friction:** Constantino had a bruising primary against Smullen. Stefanik endorsed him but has made no joint appearances. The DEC and illegal-dumping allegations about a Lisbon farm are mentioned in Daily Gazette links. The Times Union ran "Constantino controversy clouds Republicans' chances", which I could not read because it is paywalled.
- **Vance rally:** VP Vance is set to rally with Constantino in Rome on Monday, Nov 2 (Daily Gazette, Oct 29).
- **Endorsement:** Border Patrol unions endorsed Constantino on Oct 30 (Breitbart).

## Reasoning
- The only non-partisan October poll (Siena) has Gendebien +4. Democratic-sponsored polls in late September and October all show Constantino +2 to +3, which is unusual for D internals and suggests the true margin is about even or slightly Constantino.
- The baseline is Trump +21. Apply about a 10-point national swing and the seat is still about R+10.
- Two things pull the other way: Constantino has a weak profile (39/52 favourability), and Republican consolidation was incomplete in the Siena poll. Undecided Republicans in a Trump +21 seat are likely to come home.
- The money edge goes to Constantino through self-funding.
- Net: about 35% Gendebien, slightly above the market's 32.5% but within noise.

## Skeptic review (2026-11-01)
**Verdict: upheld (market about right).** Skeptic q_underdog (Gendebien) = 0.34. Kalshi has Gendebien at 0.325.

_Method note: the shared WebSearch budget ran out after one search, so this review is based on direct WebFetch checks of the cited pages, Wikipedia, and data/clean. No new searches were run for news from the last 72 hours._

**Claims checked**
- Siena (Times Union/WPTZ/WAMC), 496 LV: Gendebien 46, Constantino 42. About 85% of Democrats back Gendebien; about 65% of Republicans back Constantino, with 10% of Republicans undecided (WCAX, Oct 12 2026). The Daily Gazette (Oct 29) dates its release to Oct 9 and gives Constantino 39/52 favourability. https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/12/poll-shows-tight-race-elise-stefaniks-congressional-seat/
- The Vance rally is at the Skydome Hangar in **Rome**, Mon Nov 2, 9:15 a.m., not Ogdensburg as earlier reports said. Stefanik endorsed Constantino online but has made no joint appearance and none is planned (Daily Gazette, Oct 29 2026). Earlier Stefanik margins were 62-38 (2024) and 59-40 (2022).
- Money (Yahoo/Daily Gazette, Oct 27 2026): Constantino has $2.29M cash on hand, spent $1.19M Oct 1-14, has loaned himself $17M, and moved $640k to the NY GOP federal committee. Save American Freedom PAC is running anti-Gendebien TV and digital ads (no amount given).
- The local polls file confirms the Impact Research (D) and PPP (D) numbers: Constantino +3, +3, +5 and +2.

**Factual errors (minor)**
- Gendebien's money: the Oct 27 article gives $7.92M total receipts and $360,011 cash on hand. The dossier's $7.66M and $364k come from the FEC file. Not material.
- J.L. Partners (Aug 15-17): the votehub-sourced row in polls_2026 records Constantino 53-40, while Wikipedia records 50-40. The jlpartners.com page itself shows no toplines.

**Missed evidence and assessment:** It is notable that Gendebien's own internal (Impact, Sep 29-Oct 4) had him behind by 3. Campaigns rarely release internals showing their candidate trailing, so the Siena +4 is plausibly the high end. On the other side, Republican consolidation in Siena is very weak (65%), the nominee's favourability is net -13, and a sitting VP is holding a final-day rally in a Trump +21 seat, which shows Republicans see real risk. These signals roughly cancel. The market at 32.5% looks fair; I have no directional edge.
