## Structure
- The Legislature restored the pre-2024 lines after *Louisiana v. Callais* (April 2026), removing Mobile from the district.
- The Black population share fell from about 49% to 40%.
- On the new lines Trump has 57.2% of the two-party vote and the PVI is R+7 (lean_2026).
- Figures won by 9.2 points in 2024 on the court-ordered map.
- Alabama has no no-excuse early voting, so there is no early-vote read.
- The race is plurality. The governor's race (Tuberville vs. Doug Jones) is on the ballot and may drive turnout.

## Reasoning
- Uniform swing (Trump +14.5, adjusted for a D+9 environment) gives about R+4.
- The polls show a tie to R+2.
- Figures has a money edge and partial incumbency, and three raters moved toward him in the final week.
- Against that, Marques leads the only nonpartisan-ish late poll (and Remington is R-leaning), and the district is structurally red.
- The market's 43.5% for Figures is close to my 46%. The gap is too small to call a mispricing.

## Skeptic review
_Independent skeptic, 2026-11-01. My web-search budget ran out partway through. The rest of this review uses direct page fetches only._

**Verdict: upheld** (market about right). Skeptic q_underdog (Figures) = 0.42.

**Claims checked**
- Remington poll confirmed: 10/23-25, 642 LV, ±4, Marques 48, Figures 46; article dated 10/29. The same poll had Tuberville leading Doug Jones by 3 within AL-02. No sponsor is stated (https://1819news.com/news/item/al-2-poll-marques-48-figures-46).
- The Tulchin, DCCC and Impact polls and the FEC figures (Figures $2.71M raised / $1.20M cash on hand; Marques $1.72M / $178k) match Wikipedia.
- AL Reporter 2026-10-29 confirms Sabato's move from Lean R to Toss-up.
- Alabama Daily News 2026-10-28 confirms: Cook moved Likely R to Lean R; AdImpact total about $10.7M; Trump renewed his endorsement on Monday; Black share about 49% before and about 40% now; Trump would have won the new district by about 14.
- Roll Call 10/31 confirms Inside moved Lean R to Tilt R.

**Errors / unverified**
- I could not verify that "Marques refused debate invitations"; the ADN 10/28 closing-pitch article does not mention debates.
- AL Reporter says the legislature "restored the district's pre-2024 boundaries." The roughly 40% Black share matches the legislature's 2023 plan, not the 2022 map. This should be noted as a definitional point.

**Missed evidence**
- Wikipedia ratings the dossier omits: DDHQ **Lean R** (10/30), The Economist **Likely R** (10/27), Silver Bulletin Likely R (stale, 8/20).
- Figures runs about 1 point ahead of Doug Jones in the same Remington poll (Figures -2, Jones -3). That suggests a small personal edge but not a decisive one.
- Racially polarized Deep South districts are inelastic. A uniform D+9 swing probably overstates Democratic gains here, which favors Marques. Alabama also has no early voting.

**Assessment.** All three D internal polls show a tie, and the only late poll, from an R-aligned firm, has Marques +2. Fundamentals give R+3 to R+5. A 42-44% price for Figures is fair, with no edge.
