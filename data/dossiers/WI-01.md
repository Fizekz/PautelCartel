## Structure
Plurality vote, 2024 map (Wisconsin's map challenge was dismissed Mar 31, 2026). Trump won the district by 4.5 in 2024 (52.3% two-party); PVI R+2. Liberal Supreme Court candidates have carried it by 6+ since 2023. Steil won by about 10 in 2024.

## Reasoning
A uniform swing from 2024 (R+2.7 nationally to about D+8.5) erases Steil's 10-point margin and leaves a coin flip, which is what the market shows. The specific evidence leans Steil:
- All three raters are at Lean R as of late October.
- The only recent polls are Democratic internals showing a tie or Berman +1. Internals typically lean about 3-4 points toward their sponsor.
- Steil has about a 2:1 advantage in total advertising.

The big Republican spending also shows the GOP sees real danger. I put P(Steil) at about 0.55. That is a modest edge, not a strong call.

## Skeptic review
*Independent skeptic, 2026-11-01. Web search budget ran out partway through; checks used WebFetch.*

**Verdict: weakened.** The Steil lean is real but smaller than the dossier says.

**Checks**
- Wikipedia's WI-01 section, read 2026-11-01, matches the dossier on polls, FEC figures and ratings: Impact Mar 48-47 Berman, DCCC 48-48, Impact Aug 49-48 Steil, Ragnar (R) 50-41. FEC through Oct 14: Steil $6.39M raised and $2.24M cash on hand; Berman $1.98M and $0.35M.
- The repo CSV notes that the Aug Impact poll was sponsored by Berman's campaign, the Sep 28-Oct 1 Impact poll by HMP, and the Ragnar poll by Conservatives For America.
- WisPolitics (2026-10-26) confirms the AdImpact totals: R $13.4M vs D $7.3M from August to Nov 3, and R $5.0M vs D $4.3M in the final two weeks.
- WPR (2026-10-22) confirms Coleman's quote, "still a bit of a favorite".

**Errors and overstatements**
- "All three main raters at Lean R" is true of Cook, Sabato and Inside. But the dossier's own repo note (ratings_2026.csv) has FiftyPlusOne leaning D and Fox, RCP, Split Ticket and Silver Bulletin at Tossup. The ratings picture is therefore mixed, not uniformly Lean R.
- The final-two-weeks spending gap is small: $5.0M vs $4.3M, about 1.16:1, not 2:1. Only the cycle total is roughly 2:1.

**Missed evidence**
- WPR (2026-10-22): Berman's fundraising has outpaced Steil's in recent months.
- WPR (2026-10-22): Cook's site says the race "looks increasingly like a close race".
- WPR (2026-10-22): Trump won the district by only about 2 in 2020 and about 4 in 2024. Steil ran 6 points ahead of the ticket in 2024, so his 10-point win is mostly a personal-vote premium.

**Skeptic view**
- Discounting the Democratic internals by about 3 points gives Steil roughly +2 to +3.
- A uniform swing from 2024 into a D+8 environment gives roughly a tie.
- Lean R ratings from Cook and Sabato in wave years have often lagged.
- P(Steil) is about 0.52.
