- Recent results: McGuire won 57.3% in 2024. Earle-Sears carried the district with 53.7% in the 2025 governor's race, even as she lost statewide by double digits.
- Independent Cooke Harvey is on the ballot (6% in the Perriello internal). Plurality rules.

## Reasoning
The district resisted the 2025 Democratic wave, still going R+7 in the governor's race, and the late-September non-partisan poll had McGuire +6 with a 54-46 Republican generic ballot. Democratic signals: three rating shifts toward Perriello in October, his money edge, strong turnout in Charlottesville and Albemarle, and a national generic ballot of D+8 to D+9. On the other side, HMP declined to spend here, and Election Day turnout erased the early-vote margin in 2025. A district fundamentals estimate of about R+4 to R+6 puts McGuire near 65-70%. The market's 64.5% is consistent with that, so I don't see a clear mispricing.

## Skeptic review
Date: 2026-11-01. Verdict: **upheld** (the market looks about right). Skeptic q_underdog (Perriello) = 0.33, against Kalshi 0.345.

**Checks**
- The Cardinal News piece of 2026-10-20 confirms the following:
  - Early vote by locality: Charlottesville +41.1, Albemarle +24.6, Lynchburg +22.8, Goochland -7.9, Powhatan -11.8; +7.0% district-wide.
  - State Navigate projects Democrats winning the early vote by 8.1.
  - "House Majority PAC currently has ads running in the 1st and 2nd districts but not the 5th."
  - It calls the WaPo poll "The only public poll from a neutral source."
- The WaPo/Schar poll (Sep 23-28, published Oct 1) is confirmed.

**Errors**
- The claim that independent expenditures are negligible is wrong. Cardinal News (2026-10-23), citing VPAP, reports that independent expenditures against McGuire total $1.2 million, more than all pro-McGuire, anti-Perriello and pro-Perriello spending combined. Cook cited liberal PAC ads "hammering McGuire on the airwaves" when it moved the race from Solid R to Likely R. https://cardinalnews.org/2026/10/23/perriello-sees-a-path-forward-in-the-5th-district-race-as-voters-grapple-with-economic-woes/

**Missed evidence**
- Further early-vote gains: Nelson +25.9 and Danville +24.4 (Cardinal, Oct 20).
- The CloseCall forecast (undated; ballot data updated Oct 27) has McGuire at 89% with a forecast margin of R+6.0, from three polls with a weighted R+5.5. https://closecallelections.com/race/2026-house-va-05-regular
- I found no October poll.

**Assessment**
The anti-McGuire outside money is a modest pro-Perriello signal the dossier missed. Against that, the only neutral poll shows McGuire +6, and the district held up for Republicans in 2025. The mid-60s price is reasonable.
