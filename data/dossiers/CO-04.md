# CO-04 — Lauren Boebert (R, inc.) vs Eileen Laubacher (D); Douglas Mangeris (L)

**Kalshi (2026-11-01 snapshot):** Boebert 0.625 (norm 0.619) / Laubacher 0.385 (KXHOUSERACE-CO04-26, vol ~$222k)
**Verdict:** market_about_right — q(Laubacher) = 0.36 — confidence: low-medium

## Polls
- Impact Research (D) for Invest in Tomorrow Coalition PAC (anti-Boebert), Sep 21–24 2026, 403 LV, ±4.8: Boebert 45, Laubacher 40, Mangeris (L) 9, undecided 6. After voters heard negative messages about Boebert ("informed ballot"): Laubacher 42, Boebert 39 (Discover DougCo). This is the only general-election poll I found.
- The On Point Politics model (updated Oct 31) gives Boebert 78% and a 4-point margin. This is a model, not a poll.

## Ratings
Cook Lean R (Oct 23; previously Solid R), Sabato Lean R (Oct 20), Inside Elections Likely R (Oct 29), DDHQ Likely R (Jul), Economist Likely R (May). Cook PVI R+9; Trump 59.4% two-party in 2024; Boebert won 2024 by about 12 points, 53.6–43.9.

## Money
- FEC through Oct 14: Laubacher raised $13.7M, $1.85M cash on hand; Boebert raised $1.23M, $274k cash on hand. That is roughly an 11:1 gap.
- Outside spending (HPPR/KUNC, 2026-10-13): Invest in Tomorrow Coalition PAC (funded by Chris Larsen) $1.5M against Boebert, including ads replaying the Beetlejuice incident. American Bridge about $400k for Laubacher. House Freedom Caucus arm about $450k for Boebert. The DCCC has not spent here.

## News
- 2026-05-16: Trump posted that he would welcome a primary challenger after Boebert campaigned for Massie. No challenger could file in time, Boebert was renominated unopposed on Jun 30, and she has since been conciliatory toward Trump.
- 2026-09-17: American Muckrakers PAC filed a sworn ethics complaint alleging relationships with staffers and a $200k payment. Boebert calls it "utterly false," and the committee has opened no investigation (Colorado Politics, Sep 23).
- 2026-10-12: The only televised debate, on 9News.
- Registration as of Sep 1: R 186,119, D 86,820, unaffiliated 274,590 (Denver Gazette, Oct 13).
- I found no CO-04 ballot-return data by party.

## Structure
2024 lines, unchanged. Plurality vote. Libertarian Mangeris polled 9%; Libertarian support usually shrinks by election day, which tends to help the Republican. Independents Veldhuizen and Thornton are minor. Colorado votes all by mail.

## Reasoning
Laubacher has a huge money advantage, Boebert has personal baggage (ethics complaint, Beetlejuice, the Trump feud), and the D+8 to D+9 national environment helps. A naive uniform swing from Boebert's 2024 underperformance gets close to 50/50. Against that, the only poll was paid for by an anti-Boebert PAC and still had her up 5. The district has a registration edge of about 100k Republicans. The DCCC has stayed out. Inside Elections still says Likely R. In 2018 the old CO-04 barely swung. The market's 38.5% for Laubacher already reflects a strong wave. If anything it slightly overrates her, but not enough to call the price wrong. I put Laubacher at 0.36.
