## Reasoning
The seat leans Republican on 2024 presidential numbers. Moskowitz, however, is a well-funded incumbent with a known crossover brand, and the national environment is about D+9 (Oct 30 generic-ballot averages from DDHQ, Silver Bulletin and RCP run D+8 to D+9). The three main raters moved toward him in the last week. The dueling partisan polls average roughly Moskowitz +2 to +3, and the D polls report Trump-recall samples close to the district's real Trump +9. Florida tends to resist national Democratic swings, so about 80/20 matches the market. I see no edge.
