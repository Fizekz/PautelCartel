- Wedgewood Polls, Oct 12-14, 52-48. This appears only in the repo file (sourced from VoteHub and a Google Drive PDF). It is not on Wikipedia and could not be verified, so treat it as unconfirmed.

Missed evidence:

Every published poll is Democratic-aligned and shows +3 to +6 with Pinkins on the ballot. After a 3-4 point sponsor discount, polls and fundamentals converge on Hyde-Smith +7-10.
