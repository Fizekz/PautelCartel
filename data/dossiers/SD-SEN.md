## Structure
Effectively a two-way race between the incumbent and an independent (Bengs was the 2022 Democratic nominee) in an R+15 state. A withdrawn Democrat may still be on the ballot and pull a little of the anti-Rounds vote. No RCV.

## Judgment
**market_about_right. q_underdog (Bengs) = 0.05. Confidence: medium.**

Independent polls (Mason-Dixon +19, Emerson +8) and the averages (+10) all favour Rounds. Only D-sponsored Concord shows Bengs ahead. Bengs has almost no cash left and much of the state still does not know him. Nebraska 2024 (Osborn) showed that this kind of race tightens in polls but still falls short, which argues against assuming the polls overstate Rounds. A 6.5% price is about fair.

## Skeptic review (2026-11-01)
**Verdict: upheld (market_about_right). Skeptic q_underdog (Bengs) = 0.05.**

Note: the shared WebSearch budget was used up. This review uses direct fetches only, and SD Searchlight and Ballotpedia returned nothing usable.

**Claims checked**
- **Concord Oct 22-26:** 448 LV, Bengs 43, Rounds 41, undecided 6, MoE ±4.63, per the Concord release dated Oct 26 (https://concordpublicopinionpartners.org/polls/south-dakota-ge-2026-oct26). The release names no sponsor. Wikipedia says Democrats for Education Reform, and the earlier Oct 8 release was "for Education Reform Now Advocacy". Data issue: polls_2026.csv also has a Wikipedia-sourced row with other_pct=52 for this poll. That row is a parsing artefact; the true Bengs figure is 43.
- **Mason-Dixon:** article dated Oct 22, 2026, fielded Oct 12-14, 500 respondents, Rounds 56, Bengs 37. 34% did not recognise Bengs (https://www.sdnewswatch.org/south-dakota-poll-us-senate-rounds-bengs). Small error: the article's methods section says **registered voters**, all of whom said they were likely to vote, and Wikipedia lists it as RV. The dossier says LV.
- **Quantus:** Semafor (Oct 21, 2026) reports **Rounds 57, Bengs 36** and gives no field dates or sample. The 59-37 figure is Wikipedia's leaner-pushed version, Oct 17-19, 633 LV. The dossier attributes 59-37 to the Semafor URL, which is inaccurate.
- **Other polls:** Emerson (50-42), POS/Rounds (54-37) and Zenith (44-38 / 53-47) match Wikipedia.
- **FEC:** matches (Rounds $1.30M cash on hand; Bengs $69,708).
- **Beaudion:** withdrew Aug 4. KOTA (Aug 6, 2026) confirms he endorsed no one and that SD Democrats would not name a replacement before the Aug 11 deadline. His ballot status is still unconfirmed. Kalshi lists him at 0.005, which suggests his name may still appear on the ballot. That could split the anti-Rounds vote, which hurts Bengs.

**Missed evidence**
- Rounds declined debate invitations from SD Public Broadcasting and KELOLAND, according to the Bengs campaign (Dakota News Now, Oct 9, 2026, https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2026/10/09/bengs-challenges-rounds-public-debate-before-election-day/).
- The dossier left out a Bengs-campaign PPP poll, Sep 8-9, 629 RV: Rounds 45-42 (Wikipedia).
- PollCheck (updated 1 Nov): Rounds 51.5, Bengs 41.2, **96% Rounds win probability**, Safe R (https://www.pollcheck.co.uk/us-midterms-2026/senate/south-dakota). That is slightly above Kalshi's 93-94%.
- Split Ticket moved to Likely R on Oct 30. Every other rater has Safe or Solid R.

**Assessment**
Only the D-aligned Concord polls show Bengs ahead. All nonpartisan and R polls show Rounds up 8 to 22. Bengs has no money left and a third of voters do not recognise him. I found nothing that supports an upset call, and 5 to 6.5% is fair.
