# CO-03: Rep. Jeff Hurd (R) vs Dwayne Romero (D)

**Kalshi (2026-11-01):** Hurd (R) is the favourite at 0.625. Romero is at 0.375. Event volume is about $130k.
**View:** market_underrates_favourite (mild). q(Romero wins) = 0.30. Confidence: low-medium.

## Structure
2024 map, no change. All-mail voting. Trump won the district by about 10 in 2024, and Hurd won by about 5. An independent, Heather Barton, filed paperwork; I could not confirm she is on the ballot.

## Judgment
The national environment, about D+8-9, makes a Trump+10 seat competitive on uniform swing. Several model-based raters call it a toss-up. Against that:
- The only poll is a Democratic internal showing D+1. Released internals usually lean toward their sponsor.
- Democrats are visibly not investing, and HMP moved its money to CO-08. That is revealed-preference evidence about private data.
- Hurd has a large money edge and incumbency as a moderate.
- In 2018, R incumbents rated Likely R by Cook won 26 of 28.

I put Hurd at about 70%, a little above the market's 62.5%.

## Skeptic review
**Verdict: weakened.** The direction (Hurd at or above the market) is plausible, but the case rests on base rates and on reading money as a signal, with no independent polls. My q(Romero) is 0.34; the dossier had 0.30.

Method note: by the time of this review (2026-11-01) the shared WebSearch budget was used up, so I could not run fresh searches. I checked the dossier's own sources with WebFetch and recomputed base rates from data/clean (ratings_hist.csv, results_house.csv, lean_2026.csv). Anything in the last 3 weeks that is not linked below went unchecked.

**Verified**
- Colorado Sun, 2026-10-27 (https://coloradosun.com/2026/10/27/dwayne-romero-jeff-hurd-super-pac-spending/): more than $3M spent to benefit Hurd. Safety & Affordability and No Going Back together account for $2.5M, including about $1M of anti-Romero TV on Oct 19. Democratic IEs were only about $15k. HMP plans to use about $300k of a roughly $1M Colorado reservation and to move the rest to CO-08. The DCCC added CO-03 to its targets in early October. No independent poll exists.
- The 2018 base rate holds: Cook Likely R Republican incumbents won 26 of 28. Pooling 2010, 2014 and 2018, Likely-rated seats of the president's party held 56 of 59.
- FEC through Oct 14: Hurd raised $4.17M and has $0.24M cash on hand; Romero raised $1.70M and has $0.20M (fec_2026.csv).

**Errors / cautions**
- I could not read the DCCC memo PDF (compressed streams). Its metadata says it was created 2026-10-23, and its embedded images carry "Made with Google AI" C2PA tags. The 47-46 topline comes only from Wikipedia (polls_2026.csv) and the Sun's "up 1". The tags probably cover the charts only, but treat the memo's details as unverified.
- "Democrats visibly not investing" is overstated. Aspen Public Radio (2026-10-30) says that after the DCCC poll came out, "a national super PAC dropped $2.1 million into the race", without naming the PAC or its side. If it is HMP or another Democratic group, it reverses the HMP-retreat signal. If it is the Republican side, it adds to Hurd's $3M+. The dossier does not mention it.
- The Mesa County ballot returns (Republicans about 2:1) are the usual pattern, as the dossier itself says, so they carry no information.

**Missed evidence**
- Uniform swing points both ways. On Trump 2024 (Democratic two-party 45.0%, lean_2026.csv) plus about 5 points, the seat is about 50/50. Hurd's 2024 House result (Frisch at 47.4% two-party, results_house.csv) plus the same House-vote swing gives Romero a slight edge before incumbency. That supports the market more than 0.70 does.
- Pointing the other way: in 2018, a D+8.6 year, Tipton held the old CO-03 with 54.2% two-party (Mitsch Bush 45.8%, results_house.csv), on lines Trump won by about 12. The rural Western Slope seat has been inelastic, though the lines changed in 2022.
- The market prices nearly every Lean or Likely R seat at about 0.55-0.70 (Kalshi snapshot: CO-04 0.625, NC-03 0.555, NC-13 0.595, NJ-02 0.705). Hurd at 0.625 is a board-wide bet on a big wave, not a race-specific mistake. The "underrates favourite" view is in effect a bet that 2018-style rater calibration still holds.
