# NC-09: Rep. Richard Hudson (R, NRCC chair) vs Richard Ojeda (D)

**Kalshi, Nov 1:** Hudson (REP) 0.665, Ojeda (DEM) 0.335. Event volume is about $487k.
**Judgement:** market underrates the favourite (Hudson). q(Ojeda) = 0.25. Confidence: medium.

## Structure
- Hudson is the incumbent. North Carolina redrew its map in October 2025, but only NC-01 changed; NC-09 is unchanged per lean_2026.
- 2024 presidential vote was Trump 58.0 / Harris 42.0 two-party (about Trump +16). PVI is R+8.
- 2024 House result: Hudson 56.3%, Bristow 37.8%.
- The race is a plurality two-way.

## Polls
Both public polls are Democratic-sponsored, and both show Hudson +4:
- **PPP for House Majority PAC, Oct 19-20, 526 LV:** Hudson 46, Ojeda 42, 12% undecided (Carolina Journal; WRAL).
- **Lake Research (Ojeda campaign), Aug 31-Sep 3, 400 LV:** Hudson 46, Ojeda 42 (polls_2026; Washington Sun, Oct 8).
- I found no independent or GOP poll.
- For context, the NC Senate race polls at Cooper (D) +7 to +14 in October, against Trump +3.2 in NC in 2024.

## Ratings
- **Cook:** Likely R as of Oct 30. The Washington Sun said Cook was still Solid R on Oct 8 (https://www.washingtonsun.com/campaigns/house-republican-north-carolina-spending), so Cook moved it Solid to Likely between Oct 8 and Oct 30. I could not pin the exact date.
- **Sabato:** Safe R (Oct 27).
- **Inside Elections:** Likely R (Oct 29).
- **Others:** Fox Safe R, RCP Lean R, the rest Likely R.

## Money
- **Candidate FEC to Oct 14:** Hudson raised $4.17M and had $926k cash on hand. Ojeda raised $2.93M and had $45.6k cash on hand.
- WRAL reported end-of-September cash of Ojeda about $154k vs Hudson $1.6M.
- Hudson reserved more than $200k in TV for himself (Washington Sun, Oct 8). The NRCC has begun funding ads for him (WRAL).
- The DCCC launched its first NC-09 TV ad, "Corrupt", in the Greensboro market in late October; the amount was not disclosed (Carolina Journal; https://dccc.org/dccc-releases-first-tv-ad-in-nc-09-corrupt/).
- I found no reporting of HMP TV spending here.

## News
- Aug 5: DCCC added NC-09 to its "Districts in Play" list.
- Oct 14: Ojeda's first TV ad attacked Hudson over pharma donations (Washington Examiner, Oct 14).
- Ojeda campaigns against the Iran war, which Hudson supports (WRAL, late October).
- North Carolina early vote shows GOP turnout down and Dem/unaffiliated turnout up (WRAL). The article gives no district figures. In-person early voting ended Oct 31.

## Reasoning
- The only polls are D internals at Hudson +4 with 12% undecided. D internals typically flatter their side by 3-5 points, which implies Hudson roughly +6 to +9.
- A uniform national swing (about 10 points) applied to Trump +16 gives about Hudson +6. The bigger NC swing in the Cooper race partly reflects Cooper's personal strength.
- Ojeda is essentially out of cash, and no reported big outside D money has come in.
- Raters sit at Likely or Safe R. Likely-R seats historically flip well under 25% of the time, even in waves.
- The 33.5% market price looks rich for Ojeda. I estimate about 25%.

## Skeptic review (2026-11-01)
**Verdict: weakened ("market underrates favourite" not established).** Skeptic q_underdog (Ojeda) = 0.29. Kalshi has Ojeda at 0.335.

_Method note: the shared WebSearch budget ran out after one search, so this review is based on direct WebFetch checks of the cited pages, Wikipedia, and data/clean. No new searches were run for news from the last 72 hours._

**Claims checked**
- PPP for HMP, Oct 19-20, 526 LV: Hudson 46, Ojeda 42, 12% undecided. Confirmed by Carolina Journal (Oct 30 2026), WRAL (posted Oct 23, updated Oct 26) and the current Wikipedia page. Lake Research, Aug 31-Sep 3, 400 LV: also 46-42-12 (Wikipedia).
- FEC to Oct 14: Hudson $4.17M raised, $926k cash on hand; Ojeda $2.93M raised, $45.6k cash on hand. Confirmed (Carolina Journal; data/clean/fec_2026.csv). Hudson's $200k+ TV reservation as of Oct 8 confirmed (Carolina Journal).
- **Cook timing is more recent than the dossier says.** WRAL (posted Oct 23, updated Oct 26 2026) still listed Cook at "Solid Republican" and said "National Democrats have not yet spent in Hudson's district but could". Wikipedia now shows Cook Likely R dated Oct 30 2026. So Cook moved toward Ojeda around Oct 27-30, and the DCCC's first TV ad ("Corrupt", Carolina Journal Oct 30) came in the same window. https://www.wral.com/news/nccapitol/nc-democrat-republican-turnout-midterm-us-house-senate-flip-red-district-hudson-ojeda-october-2026/ ; https://www.carolinajournal.com/democrats-launch-tv-ad-targeting-hudson-days-before-election/
- WRAL also says statewide GOP early-vote turnout is "down" while Democratic and independent turnout is "way up", but it gives no figures. It describes HMP as "boosting Ojeda" and doesn't say how.
- Civitas (John Locke Foundation) rates the seat R+7, "likely Republican" (Carolina Journal, Oct 30).
- Wikipedia dates Sabato's Safe R to Feb 6 2025, which looks stale, and DDHQ's Likely R to Jul 14 2026.

**Factual errors:** The Cook rating history is mis-timed. Cook was Solid R until at least Oct 23-26, not only "as of Oct 8", so the upgrade is a late signal (within the last ~5 days), not a month-old one. Sabato "Safe R (Oct 27)" is the file's snapshot date; Wikipedia gives the rating's own date as Feb 2025.

**Why the claim is weakened:** The case for Hudson rests on discounting two D-sponsored polls. But the late signals all point the other way: Cook's move, the first DCCC TV money in the final week, and NC's large statewide swing in the Senate race (the dossier itself cites ~13 points; Trump +16 minus 13 is about R+3). Democrats don't usually spend new money in the final week on a race their data shows as Hudson +8. Ojeda's low cash is real, and Hudson as NRCC chair can call in support. Overall the market looks at most 3-5 points rich on Ojeda, which is within noise and not a tradable edge. Hudson is still the right pick.
