## Structure
NC 2025 mid-decade redraw (new 2026 lines) moved GOP coastal areas to NC-1 and added Wayne, Wilson, Greene and Lenoir counties. Trump +14 on the new lines (D 2p 43.1%). Black VAP rose from 20% to 29%. Much of the new territory has not had Murphy as its incumbent. Libertarian Daniel Cavender is also on the ballot.

## Judgement
Kalshi and Polymarket (55/44, PredScope Nov 1) both now price this as near a coin flip. Earlier Kalshi snapshots were ~84-91%, and Polymarket was 70% on Oct 24. The move appears driven by NC early-vote composition and the Trump/Vance visits. But every rater with full information still has Likely R or safer, and Inside only moved it off Solid on Oct 31. Smith has raised a tenth of Murphy's total and has $44k cash. A Trump+14 seat with a 12-point statewide swing still comes out around R+2, before Murphy's personal vote. Early-vote party registration in eastern NC overstates D strength because of conservative registered Democrats, and Republicans vote late. I estimate Murphy ~0.68, so the market underrates him. The main risk is a genuine wave plus surging Black turnout.

**Assessment**
The main pro-Murphy points hold up. There is no public poll since August, and that one (Murphy +3, D-sponsored) predates the environment's late shift. Smith has only $44k in cash and no national D investment. All raters are at Likely R or Lean R.

On the other side, the district is now Trump +14 with 29% Black VAP. The statewide CJ generic is D+9 against NC's 2024 Trump +3, a ~12-point swing, which puts the seat near R+2. NC-3 early ballots run about 2:1 D over R. A deep, heavy-volume market has priced all of this in.

I lean Murphy, about 0.61. That is less than the dossier's 0.68, and the evidence for a big edge over 0.555 is thin.
