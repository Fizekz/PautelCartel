## Reasoning
This is an elastic Long Island seat in a roughly D+9 environment, so a pure swing model makes it look closer than R+6 suggests, and the market's 17.5% for Halpin reflects that. Against it: there are no polls, no rating moves, no national Democratic investment, and Halpin has about $49k in the bank versus Garbarino's $2.7M. Garbarino also has a record of running well ahead of the top of the ticket. I estimate about 14%, slightly below the market. That is not a strong enough signal to call the price wrong.
