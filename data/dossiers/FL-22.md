- I found no district-level early or mail vote data.

## Structure
Newly drawn in DeSantis's mid-decade map (signed 2026-05-04). It stretches across the state through Palm Beach, Broward (Weston, Davie), Hendry and Collier (Marco Island). It is effectively an open seat: incumbent Lois Frankel runs in the new FL-23. Trump won 55.2-44.8 two-party in 2024 (lean_2026.csv); Cook PVI R+4. Plurality race.

## Judgment
A Trump +10 seat with an R+8 registration edge is about even in a D+8 to D+9 environment. That matches the three major raters (Toss-up) and the Democratic super PAC's own late poll (+0.8). DDHQ's model has Askar favoured at 58%, while Split Ticket and Silver Bulletin lean D. Askar has more cash and roughly $6.5M of MAGA Inc.-funded air cover. Florida has also under-swung toward Democrats relative to the nation in recent cycles. Nothing I found supports Dandiya at 71.5%, and the market is very thin ($181K). A fair price is roughly 52-58% Dandiya. I set q(Askar) = 0.45. Of the five races in this batch, this is the clearest case of a mispriced underdog.

## Skeptic review
_Independent skeptic, 2026-11-01. The session web-search budget ran out before this review began, so no new searches were possible. Every check below came from fetching the cited URLs and a few primary pages directly (Wikipedia and pollster pages), plus repo side data._

**Verdict: UPHELD (market_underrates_underdog).** Skeptic q(Askar) = 0.43, against Kalshi's 0.285.

**Claims verified**
- RedBox Research poll for A Fight Worth Having (D super PAC), Oct 20-23, n=550 LV, MoE 5.1: Dandiya 46.3, Askar 45.5; independents 56.8-24.9. DDHQ model has Askar at 58% as of Oct 23. Source: hiindia, 2026-10-28.
- Impact Research for HMP, Aug 24-31: 46-44, informed ballot 49-44, generic ballot 46-46. Source: American Bazaar, 2026-09-27.
- Inside Elections moved the race from Tilt R to Toss-up. Source: Roll Call, 2026-10-31.
- The district was Trump +10, with about an 8-point R registration edge and Amendment 4 winning by about 20. Source: WLRN, 2026-10-22.
- Cook, Sabato and Inside all rate it Toss-up; Split Ticket and Silver Bulletin rate it Lean D. Source: ratings_2026.csv, as of 2026-10-30.

**Errors and unverified claims**
- "No Going Back PAC spent about $6.5M per FEC" is not verified. WLRN (2026-10-22) says a Trump-tied super PAC had spent "more than $3.76 million" against Dandiya (midtermmoney.com). The $6.5M figure may be more recent, but I found no source for it.
- polls_2026.csv labels the Aug 24-31 poll "Tulchin Research", while American Bazaar says Impact Research. This is a side-data inconsistency, not a dossier error.
- The Tavern Research Aug 10-12 poll (50-50) is tagged D-sponsored in polls_2026.csv. The dossier lists it without that label.
- The Floridian Press article (2026-10-27) is a conservative-leaning outlet. Its "Askar surges" claim rests on unnamed earlier polls, so it carries little weight.

**Missed evidence (favours Askar)**
- Florida statewide generic ballot polls:
  - NYT/Siena, Oct 19-25, n=813 LV: R 47, D 42.
  - Univision/YouGov, Oct 2-15, n=825 LV: R 49, D 44.
  - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Florida (fetched 2026-11-01).
- What that implies for FL-22:
  - Florida was R+13.2 two-party in 2024 (lean_2026.csv, FL-SEN row), and FL-22 was R+10.4, about 2.8 points more Democratic than the state.
  - A statewide generic ballot of R+5 therefore puts the district near R+2 on uniform swing.
  - This is the concrete evidence behind the dossier's "Florida under-swings" point, which it did not source.
- Only partly offset by: the Amendment 4 margin and Split Ticket and Silver Bulletin at Lean D.

**Assessment.** Both the district fundamentals and the only October poll (a Democratic one) point to a coin flip or a slight Askar edge, and DDHQ's model agrees. I found nothing that supports 71.5% for Dandiya. The $181K market is thin. The refutation attempt failed.
