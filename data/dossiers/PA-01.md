**Assessment**
The one quality poll has Harvie +2 with n=400, which is a coin flip. The two D-internal ties are, if anything, mildly good for Fitzpatrick, because released D internals usually flatter the Democrat. Against that, Fitzpatrick has to run about 27 points ahead of the governor's margin in his district. His crossover record makes that plausible, but this year it is not likely.

I see no evidence of mispricing in either direction. 0.535/0.465 is fair, perhaps a touch generous to Harvie.
