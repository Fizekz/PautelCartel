## Reasoning
Uniform swing from Trump +15 under a generic ballot of about D+8.5 leaves Salazar ahead by about 4 to 5 points. Hispanic voters swinging back toward Democrats in 2025 (for example, Higgins winning the Miami mayoralty) could narrow that further. The only polls are Democratic-sponsored and show a 0-1 point race, which after a typical sponsor lean points to Salazar up a few. Salazar has distanced herself from Trump on immigration, holds the cash edge and skipped debates, which is a front-runner's strategy. Ratings have been static for 5 weeks. A price of 63.5% for Salazar matches this. No specific late evidence (an independent poll or district early-vote data) argues for moving it.

## Skeptic review
Date: 2026-11-01. Verdict: **upheld** (the market looks about right). Skeptic q_underdog (Rodriguez) = 0.35, against Kalshi 0.365.

**Errors**
- The dossier says "No poll fielded after Sep 15 was found." That is wrong. A Fight Worth Having, a super PAC (FEC C00933085, donations via ActBlue), released a RedBox Research poll on 2026-10-25.
  - Fieldwork: Oct 20-23, n=523 LV, MoE ±5.5, text-to-web.
  - Results: Salazar 49.1, Rodriguez 46.4. In the same sample, Moody led 47.6-45.0 and Donalds 47.2-45.9.
  - https://afightworthhaving.com/polling/2026/fl-27

**Missed evidence**
- CiberCuba, 2026-10-31: on Oct 30 Trump gave Salazar a "Full and Total Endorsement for Reelection" on Truth Social and called Rodriguez "reckless, radical, and dangerous." This reverses the September attacks the dossier cites, so the risk of Republican base defection is lower.
  - The same article says VoteHub's model has Salazar at 67.4%, and that more than 235,000 early votes had been cast in Miami-Dade as of Oct 24.
  - https://en.cibercuba.com/noticias/2026-10-31-u1-e208512-s27061-nid344433-trump-respalda-maria-elvira-salazar-congresista-27
- Bill Clinton endorsed Rodriguez on 2026-10-28 (same CiberCuba source).

**Assessment**
The only late poll is non-campaign but possibly Democratic-aligned, and it has Salazar ahead by 2.7. Trump's reconciliation removes a downside risk. Together these support a price in the mid-60s. There is no evidence of a mispricing.
