# lean_2026.csv: partisan lean of every 2026 race, on the lines used in November 2026

Built by `src/data/fetch_lean.py` (run `.venv/bin/python src/data/fetch_lean.py`; add `--refresh` to re-download).
Pulled 2026-11-01. 470 rows: 435 House and 35 Senate, joined on the race_ids in
`data/templates/full_congress_template.csv`. Every race_id is present.

## Columns

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| race_id, chamber | key from the template (`chamber` is `House` / `Senate`) |
| pres24_dem_2p, pres24_rep_2p | Harris and Trump shares of the two-party vote in 2024, on the **2026** district lines (Senate: statewide) |
| pres20_dem_2p | Biden's two-party share in 2020 on the 2026 lines. **Blank for the 145 districts redrawn for 2026** (see gaps) |
| cook_pvi | Cook PVI text (`R+5`, `D+3`, `EVEN`), taken from the Wikipedia table "Cook Partisan Voting Index" (rev 1374239063, which calls itself the 2026 Cook PVI and already shows the new 2026 maps) |
| pvi_num | cook_pvi as a number, D positive (`R+5` -> -5, `EVEN` -> 0) |
| lines | `2024-lines` (state map unchanged since 2024), `new-2026-lines` (state redrew for 2026), `statewide` (Senate) |
| source | where the row's numbers came from |
| pvi_calc | *extra.* Our own Cook-style PVI, D positive, 1 decimal: 0.75 x (d24 - nat24) + 0.25 x (d20 - nat20), in points, on two-party shares. National two-party D share from MIT: 2024 0.49250, 2020 0.52268. Blank where pres20 is blank |
| district_changed_2026 | *extra.* 1 if the district's 2026 geography differs from the same-numbered 2024 district |

## Redistricting for 2026

- **New maps used in November 2026 (9 states): AL, CA (Prop 50), FL, LA, NC, OH, TN, TX, UT.** All of their districts
  are labelled `new-2026-lines`. Of those 173 districts, **145 actually changed** (CA 49, TX 37, FL 21, OH 14, TN 8,
  LA 6, AL 4, UT 4, NC 2). That matches The Downballot's own count ("145 districts that did change"). The other 28 have
  2024 vote totals identical to the same-numbered 2024 district, so we treat them as unchanged (`district_changed_2026=0`)
  and fill their pres20 from the 2024-lines sheet: AL-03/04/05, CA-11/15/37, FL-01..07, NC-02, NC-04..14, OH-11, TN-01, TX-19.
- **Missouri is on its 2022/2024 map.** The legislature passed a new map in 2025, and the Downballot's July post still
  counts MO among the "10 states". But the U.S. Supreme Court ordered the 2022 map used for 3 Nov 2026 (KCUR, 2026-09-29,
  https://www.kcur.org/podcast/up-to-date/2026-09-29/missouri-proposition-a-congressional-map-2026-election), and
  Proposition A on the 2025 map is on the same ballot. The live Downballot sheet shows MO's 8 districts identical to
  2024, so MO is `2024-lines` and has pres20. If anyone uses an older snapshot that has MO's 2025 map, MO-05 would be
  wrong (D+12 on the 2022 lines).
- Kalshi cross-check: the shapes agree with the new lines. For example UT-01 (D+12, new Salt Lake seat) has the
  Democrat at 0.98, and CA-22 (D+1 after Prop 50) has the Democrat favoured.

## Checks

- The Wikipedia Cook PVI and our pvi_calc agree on all 290 districts that have both years: mean |diff| 0.24, max 0.6.
  The leftover differences are .5 rounding ties. That match also confirms Cook's 75/25 weighting (a 50/50 weighting
  misses 110 districts).
- For the 145 changed districts we cannot check cook_pvi against 2020, because pres20 is blank. Against the 2024-only
  relative share the gap is mean -0.15, sd 0.67, max 2.1, which is consistent with Cook adding 2020 on the new lines.
- The script fails if: the table does not have 470 unique rows, any pres24 is missing or outside 5-95%, or the Wikipedia
  parse does not find exactly 435 districts and 50 states.

## Gaps and caveats

- **pres20_dem_2p is blank for the 145 redrawn districts.** The Downballot has not published 2020 on the new lines, and
  we did not compute it ourselves (Dave's Redistricting has it per map, but it is not a bulk download). Use pres24,
  cook_pvi, or `pvi_num` (which has 2020 baked in) for those districts.
- cook_pvi is a Wikipedia transcription of a paywalled source. Our own calculation agrees with it to within rounding
  wherever we can check. Wikipedia can be edited, so the revision id is pinned in `source`.
- Senate rows are statewide presidential results. FL-SEN and OH-SEN are the 2026 specials; their lean is the same as
  any statewide race.
- Presidential lean says nothing about: uncontested races (several safe seats have no major-party opponent), top-two
  general elections (CA and WA can have D-vs-D or R-vs-R contests), ranked-choice (AK, ME), or independents (for
  example NE-SEN, and CA-03, whose incumbent left the GOP). These need the candidate tables.
- 2024 values are two-party shares, not margins. The margin is `2*share - 1`.

## Independent verification (second agent, 2026-11-01)

- Re-ran the script offline: output byte-identical. 470 rows match the template exactly (no extra, missing or
  duplicated race_ids, chamber agrees). pres24 D+R = 1 exactly; pvi_num sign always matches cook_pvi.
- Every district outside AL/CA/FL/LA/NC/OH/TN/TX/UT has identical 2024 shares
  in the 2026-lines and 2024-lines sheets (MO included), so the `lines` labels are internally consistent. MO on its
  2022 map confirmed by an independent source (Missouri Supreme Court 2026-09-03 ruling and the U.S. Supreme Court
  order of late Sept 2026, via KSMU/St. Louis Public Radio/ADN reports). `data/clean/redistricting_2026.csv`
  (built separately from Wikipedia) lists the same 9 states.
- Spot checks against non-Downballot sources: ME-01 0.61042 = official 258,863/165,214; NE-02 Harris +4.6 (Wikipedia,
  official) vs +4.7 two-party here; UT-01 Harris +24 (Inside Elections) vs +24.3; CA-22 Trump +2 and CA-48 Harris +4
  (Ballotpedia/KQED on Prop 50) vs -1.9 / +3.5; FL-14 Castor seat Trump 55-44 (WUSF) vs 55.3/44.7 two-party;
  CA-01 Harris double digits vs +12.6; NC-01 Trump +11.6, OH-09 Trump +10.7, TX-28/34/35 Trump +10 to +11 all match
  press figures. At-large rows (AK, DE, ND, SD, VT, WY) equal the MIT statewide shares. All 35 Senate rows match
  MIT statewide 2024/2020.
- Cook PVI itself could not be fetched (cookpolitical.com returns 403), so cook_pvi is still checked only through the
  internal 75/25 recomputation (290 districts within 0.6).
- Kalshi join (all 470 present): correlation of pvi_num with Kalshi D probability 0.86 (excluding CA top-two UNK
  rows). No race has an R favourite in a D-leaning (>= EVEN) seat. 30-odd R+2..R+8 seats and AK/OH/TX-SEN have the
  Democrat favoured, consistent with a D-leaning 2026 environment, not with a data error. CA-04/07/11/12/14/29/34/37
  and CA-40 are same-party top-two races where presidential lean says nothing about the winner.
